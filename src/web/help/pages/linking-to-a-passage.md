---
title: Linking to a passage
summary: How to copy a link that opens the article at the same place in the same mode, or at one exact paragraph.
keywords: share link url send copy permalink paragraph passage address bar open at mode reload where i left
---

The address bar always holds where you are and how you are looking: the section on your screen, the
open mode, and what is picked inside it, such as a glossary term or a Skim stop. So you can copy the
address at any time and send it, and it opens at the same place in the same mode. Reloading brings
you back there too.

For one exact paragraph, press the **link** icon in its margin. That copies a link that opens at
that paragraph and keeps your current mode. On a phone, a long press on the icon offers the
browser’s own **Copy link**.

Opening an article from your shelf returns you to where you left it on that device, but a link
always wins: if someone sends you a passage, it opens at their passage. When signed in, an article
you have not opened in this browser before can open in Summary, where the window has room for it
beside the text.

The person you send it to can open it only if they can see the article — see
[Sharing](/help/sharing).

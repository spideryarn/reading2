---
title: Why did the person icon gain a pencil after I edited my profile?
keywords: out of date stale label written for you regenerate profile changed
---

Nothing broke. Each personalised result records which version of your profile it was written for,
and changing even one letter can make that an older version. Nothing is rewritten unless you ask.
Press the icon to see the profile and, where that mode can replace its result, the Regenerate
button. See [Your reader profile](/help/reader-profile).

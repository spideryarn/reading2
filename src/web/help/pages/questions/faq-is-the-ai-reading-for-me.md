---
title: Is the AI reading the article for me?
keywords: summary replace reading trust grounded passages sources accurate hallucinate made up check
---

No. It is there to help you read the article, not to read it instead of you. Most of what the modes
show points back to the article’s own passages, so you can check it in one click, and quotes are cut
from the article itself.

Treat a summary as a map, not the territory: when something matters, follow the link and read the
passage. See [the AI’s words and the author’s](/help/ai-words).

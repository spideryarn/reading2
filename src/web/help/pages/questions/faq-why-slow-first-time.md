---
title: Why does a mode take a while the first time, and not the second?
keywords: slow wait loading spinner first time instant fast generating stored minutes
---

Most modes are one pass of the AI over the whole article. It runs the first time anyone asks, and
the result is stored with the article, so after that the mode opens straight away — for you and for
anyone you share it with. To skip the first wait, leave **Generate the main modes as soon as it
opens** ticked when you add an article. See [Waiting for a mode](/help/waiting-and-cost).

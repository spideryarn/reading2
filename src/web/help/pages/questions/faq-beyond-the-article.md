---
title: Does it ever look beyond the article?
keywords: go web internet search outside sources other articles knowledge where does the answer come from
---

Sometimes, and it tells you when. Chat can reach the open web, your other saved articles, or a page
this one links to, and any answer that did has a strip above it saying so.
[Debate](/help/mode-debate) is all about what the rest of the web says, and every row links to its
source. Glossary’s **Dig deeper**, Citations’ **Dig deeper** and Referee’s **Candidates** can also
search or fetch outside sources. The glossary’s **background** notes and Citations’ influence bars
come from the model’s general knowledge (a row says *influence unknown* where no usable influence
score was saved; new lists ask the model to leave it unknown when unsure; a bar marked *from the
web* is instead an AI estimate from a page Dig deeper found), and a reader profile can shape how
some aids are written. Where the article gives a work’s DOI, Citations also looks the work up in
Crossref or DataCite, the registries that issue DOIs, for its authors and year, and a row that says
*cited 357 times · Crossref* is showing Crossref’s own count. Most other reading aids work from the
article itself.

---
title: Does it ever look beyond the article?
keywords: go web internet search outside sources other articles knowledge where does the answer come from
---

Sometimes, and it tells you when. Chat can reach the open web, your other saved articles, or a page
this one links to, and any answer that did has a strip above it saying so.
[Sources](/help/mode-sources)’ Reception searches the web, and a Claims check does when
you press **Check**; every result links to its source. The Claims list itself comes from the article.
Referee’s **Candidates** can also search or fetch outside sources, and **Ask in chat** on a
glossary entry or a cited work starts a chat, which can. The glossary’s **background** notes and
Bibliography’s influence bars come from the model’s general knowledge (a row says *influence
unknown* where no usable influence score was saved; new lists ask the model to leave it unknown
when unsure; a bar marked *from the web* is instead an AI estimate from a page an earlier web search
found), and a reader profile can shape how some aids are written. Where the article gives a work’s
DOI, Bibliography also looks the work up in
Crossref or DataCite, the registries that issue DOIs, for its authors and year, and a row that says
*cited 357 times · Crossref* is showing Crossref’s own count. Most other reading aids work from the
article itself.

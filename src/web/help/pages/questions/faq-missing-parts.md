---
title: Why did my article come out with missing parts?
keywords: missing broken extraction images figures paywall pdf equations wrong text incomplete garbled footnotes
---

When we fetch a page we keep the article and leave behind the menus, adverts and the rest, and
sometimes a figure or a box gets thrown out with the clutter. If a paywall lets only the opening
through, the opening is all you get. A PDF is read by an AI model, which can now and then garble a
word or turn an equation into plain text; the Metadata page’s **How well we read the PDF** lists the
words it may have missed.

What to try: the shelf card’s **Re-fetch and rebuild** (or **Rebuild**, for an uploaded file) reads
the article again, without using up your allowance, and your comments stay attached. If it still
looks wrong, send [Feedback](/help/feedback) from the article with **Send extra diagnostics**
ticked, so we can see the source.

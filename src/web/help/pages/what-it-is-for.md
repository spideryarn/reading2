Spideryarn helps you read a piece properly. It does not replace it with a summary. Everything the AI
makes is there to send you back into the author’s own words: a definition points to where a term is
used, a summary paragraph links to the passages it rests on, a quote is cut straight out of the
text.

The main column is always the article itself, word for word. The AI’s work sits beside it, in the
modes, the spine and the cards, so you can tell what came from the author and what came from the
model — see [the AI’s words and the author’s](/help/ai-words).

A good way to use it: get your bearings first ([the spine](/help/spine),
[Structure](/help/mode-structure), [Skim](/help/mode-skim)), then read, stopping to ask, define or
mark things as you go. Afterwards, test what you kept. The aim is not to spend less time on the
piece, but less of it on the parts you did not need and more on the parts you did.

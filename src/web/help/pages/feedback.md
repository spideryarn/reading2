When you are signed in, the **Feedback** button is at the right-hand end of the bottom bar in the
reading view, and at the top of other pages. Pick **Problem** or **Suggestion** and write in your
own words. For a problem, the most useful things are the steps you took, what you expected and what
you saw instead. You can paste or drop in a screenshot.

It sends what you wrote, your email address, which version of Spideryarn you were running, and the
**full address of the page you were on** — so send it from the page where the problem is, and
remember that a search you had open goes with it. Tick **Send extra diagnostics** to add your recent
requests to us, the names of any errors and details of your browser; on your own article it may also
send the article’s text and source file, so we can reproduce the problem. Extra diagnostics never
include your notes, comments, chats or profile.

The **Earlier** tab lists what you have sent and the page you sent it from, and marks a report
**Shipped** once a change made for it is live. Most things that go wrong here fail quietly, so even
a two-line report helps.

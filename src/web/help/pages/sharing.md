Every article starts **private**. The padlock beside the title means it is not public. Sending
someone its ordinary address lets nobody in: copying an address changes nothing about who can open
it.

There are two ways to let other people read one, and both are under **Access & sharing** on the
article’s Metadata page: a private link, for the people you send it to, and making it public, for
everyone.

**A private link.** Press **Create a link**, look at what somebody with the link would get, tick
that you have the right to share the text, and press **Create the link**. Then copy the link and
send it however you like.

- **Anyone who has the link can read the article without an account**, and can pass the link on. We
  cannot tell you who has opened it.
- **An article shared only this way is not listed anywhere**, and the page tells whoever opens it
  that it is a private link. They get what a visitor to a public article gets, and like any visitor
  they cannot write anything or start any AI work.
- **Turn off** stops the link working from the next request. Creating a link again makes a new one,
  and the old one stays dead.
- A private link does not change what the article counts against your allowance. If the article is
  also public, its ordinary address works without the link, and turning the link off does not make
  it private.

**Making it public.** Press the padlock. It takes you to **Access & sharing** on the article’s
Metadata page, where you press **Share with anyone…**. Nothing goes out until you have seen exactly
what a visitor would get, ticked that you have the right to share the text, and pressed **Share
it**. The padlock then becomes a globe.

**While it is still being added.** You need not wait for an import to finish. The page that shows
its progress has a **Sharing options** section. It is closed until you open it, but opens itself
whenever something in it needs your attention. It has **Create a private link…** and **Make it
public…**, with the same confirmations. The copy button on the import’s card copies the article’s
ordinary address, which opens for other people only once the article is public: a private link is a
different address. Somebody who opens a shared article before its import has
finished is told it is still being added, and the page opens it when it is ready.

What sharing means:

- **Anyone can read it without an account, and it is listed** on the public shelf,
  [{{public-shelf-label}}](/read/public), so people who were never sent the link can find it. It is
  not listed in search engines.
- **Visitors get the article and the reading aids already made for it**, and **your comments and
  bookmarks** with the AI’s answers to them. They cannot write anything or start any AI work, so a
  visitor never uses up your allowance. **Your chats and your profile are not shared.**
- **An article added since 5 September 2026 counts as half while shared.** Older charged articles
  still count in full — see [Plans](/help/plans).

**Stop sharing** takes the article off the public shelf and refuses the next request for it. It
cannot take back a page somebody has already loaded or copied.

title: The margin beside each paragraph
summary: The controls that appear beside a paragraph when you point at it or tap it, and what each one does.

---

The narrow margin beside each paragraph stays empty until you point at the paragraph (or tap it on a
touchscreen). Then its controls appear:

- **Link** copies a link that opens at exactly this paragraph — see [Linking to a
  passage](/help/linking-to-a-passage).
- **Chat** opens a conversation about this paragraph, where you type your own question. If one
  already exists, the icon stays visible and shows how many.
- **Bookmark** marks the whole paragraph with one press. Nothing is written and nothing goes to the
  AI. The bookmark then stays at the top of the margin, so you can spot the paragraph later.
- **?** asks the AI to help you understand this paragraph. **One press sends it**, with no box to
  type in, and the answer opens as a chat you can carry on.
- **…** appears when a short paragraph has no room for every control.

To mark only part of a paragraph, select the words instead — see [Comments and
bookmarks](/help/comments).

On someone else’s shared article the margin can show their existing comments or bookmarks, and
always lets you copy the paragraph’s link. You cannot add a bookmark, start a chat or use the **?**.

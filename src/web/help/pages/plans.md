Your allowance counts **adding articles**, not reading them. A link you paste or a file you upload
counts once, when it comes back readable. A fetch that fails, a paywall that leaves nothing
readable, or a PDF we turn down costs nothing.

**Everything you do with an article afterwards is included**: modes, chat, questions, and re-running
a mode or a whole article already on your shelf. What each plan gives you is on [the pricing
page](/pricing).

Easy to miss:

- **The free allowance is for the life of your account**, not per month. Articles added while
  subscribed count towards it too, so cancelling doesn’t hand back a fresh one.
- **An article added since 5 September 2026 counts as half while shared**, so sharing it stretches
  your allowance; making it private again uses that half back up. An older charged article still
  counts in full, while one from before billing began already costs nothing.
- **High-powered AI**, a stronger model for one article — a tick box while it is being added, or a
  switch on its Metadata page — counts as one more article (half if the article is shared).
  Switching it off does not refund it, and switching it back on costs nothing more.
- **Papers added several at once**, with only the title, authors and abstract read, count as a
  hundredth of an article each. **Read this** reads the whole paper, and then it costs the same as
  one article in all.
- **Archiving doesn’t give an article back** to your allowance.
- **Reading is never limited.** At your limit, adding and switching another article to High-powered
  AI stop; everything you already have stays.

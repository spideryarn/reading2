**Export.** On an article’s Metadata page, **Export this article** downloads a zip of everything we
hold for it: the text as we read it, and everything built on it, such as the glossary, quotes,
comments and chats — plain files you can open without Spideryarn. The original page or PDF and the
images are not included.

**Archive is not delete.** Archive takes an article off your shelf, with an Undo for a few seconds.
Nothing is destroyed and its link still opens. Turn on **Include archived** to see archived
articles, and press **Put back** to return one.

**Deleting for good** is on the article’s Metadata page, under **Delete this article**. It erases
the article and everything you did with it, and there is no undo. It cannot reach copies already on
a device: the copy your browser keeps so the article opens offline, or an export you downloaded.

Your articles and notes are private unless you share an article. [The privacy page](/privacy) has
the full account: what we keep, where it goes, and what you can ask for, including deleting your
account.

This is for reading a piece from a field you do not know yet. Nothing here reads it for you: each
step sends you back into the author’s own words better prepared. If Spideryarn itself is new to
you, start with [Your first article](/help/first-article).

## Say what you know

- **Fill in About you** on your [reader profile](/help/reader-profile): your background, what you
  know well, what you are rusty on. It changes what gets explained and how much, never what the
  article says.
- **Answer “Why are you reading this?”** for the article. The quiz, for one, aims more of its
  questions at what you said.

## Before you read

- **Open [Ideas](/help/mode-ideas)** and read **What you need to bring**: the ideas the piece leans
  on and never states. One that is new to you explains why a passage felt opaque.
- **Open the [Glossary](/help/mode-glossary)** early. It defines terms from how this piece uses
  them, and afterwards they stay underlined in every mode.
- **See how the piece is built** with [Structure](/help/mode-structure) and [the
  spine](/help/spine): the same parts, drawn two ways.
- **Walk a [Skim](/help/mode-skim) route** for the shape of a paper. Every stop is the author’s
  sentence, in place, with a question to read it with where one helps.

## While you read

- **Read in [Plain](/help/mode-plain)**, and point at an underlined term when you need its
  definition.
- **Press the ? in [the margin](/help/gutter)** beside a paragraph you cannot follow. The answer
  opens as a [chat](/help/mode-chat) you can carry on.
- **Highlight and write notes** as you go — see [Comments and bookmarks](/help/comments).
  **Comments** in the bottom bar lists everything you have marked, in article order.
- **Try [FAQ](/help/mode-faq)** on a dense piece. It writes no answers: each question points to the
  passages that respond to it. It is one of the [experimental
  features](/help/experimental-features).

## Afterwards

- **Open [Learn](/help/mode-learn)** when you want it to stick. In **Recall** you say what you took
  from the piece and find out where you went wrong; **Tutorial** takes a little of the piece at a
  time; **Quiz** asks up to twenty short questions.
- **Go back to Ideas** for **What this piece adds**, a short list of the ideas worth taking away.
- **Read the [Summary](/help/mode-summary)** to check you came away with the main points.

When something matters, follow the link and read the passage: see [Is the AI reading the article
for me?](/help/questions#faq-is-the-ai-reading-for-me). For every other mode, see [Which mode
when](/help/modes).

This is for your first hour with Spideryarn. The idea behind all of it is in [What Spideryarn is
for](/help/what-it-is-for): it helps you read a piece properly, and does not replace it with a
summary. A good way to use it is to get your bearings first, then read, stopping to ask, define or
mark things as you go.

## Add something you want to read

- **Paste a web address** into **Add an article** on your shelf and press **Add**, or add a PDF —
  see [Adding an article](/help/adding-articles). Keep a Spideryarn tab open until it finishes.
- **Answer “Why are you reading this?”** while it is being added. Your [reader
  profile](/help/reader-profile) says what that changes.
- **If a mode is slow the first time**, that is expected: see [Waiting for a
  mode](/help/waiting-and-cost) and [why the second time is
  instant](/help/questions#faq-why-slow-first-time).

## Get your bearings

- **Start with the guide.** A new article opens with the guide beside it, where there is room: tell
  it why you are reading, and it suggests where to begin and which modes would help — see
  [Chat](/help/mode-chat).
- **Look at the screen** with [The parts of the reading view](/help/the-reading-view) to hand: the
  spine down the left edge, the article in the main column, the bottom bar with the mode buttons.
- **Point at [the spine](/help/spine)** to read a card for each section, and click to go there. It
  shows how big each part is and how far through you are.
- **Open [Summary](/help/mode-summary)** to decide whether the piece is worth your time and roughly
  where it is going. It is not there to read instead of the article.

## Read it

- **Press [Plain](/help/mode-plain)** to read straight through. Open a mode when you hit something
  you want help with, then press **Plain** to come back.
- **Point at an underlined term** to read its definition. Once the article has a
  [Glossary](/help/mode-glossary), its terms stay underlined in every mode.
- **Use [the margin beside a paragraph](/help/gutter)**: the **?** asks the AI to help you
  understand that paragraph, and the bookmark marks it with one press.
- **Select a few words** to highlight them and, if you like, write a note — see [Comments and
  bookmarks](/help/comments).
- **Ask [Chat](/help/mode-chat)** when you have a question of your own. The short codes in an answer
  link to the paragraphs it relied on, so check the passage before you trust the claim.
- **Lost after a jump?** The **↩ back to** button takes you back — see [Jumping
  around](/help/jumping-around).

## When you want more

Every bottom bar has a **Help** link near its right-hand end, and <kbd>⌘K</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl
K</kbd> opens the command bar, which takes the name of any mode — see [Keyboard
shortcuts](/help/keyboard). [Which mode when](/help/modes) lists every mode, so you can pick by the
question you have. Opening modes [does not use up your
allowance](/help/questions#faq-does-a-mode-use-my-allowance), so try them.

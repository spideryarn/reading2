This is for a piece in an area you already know. The aim, as [What Spideryarn is
for](/help/what-it-is-for) puts it, is not to spend less time on the piece, but less of it on the
parts you did not need and more on the parts you did.

## Set it up once

- **Tell the AI who is reading** in **About you** on your [reader profile](/help/reader-profile).
  It changes what gets explained, and how much: which terms the glossary thinks you need, how chat
  pitches an answer.
- **Say why you are reading this article.** Something like “I want the methods, not the history” is
  what helps.

## Find what is new

- **Walk a [Skim](/help/mode-skim) route.** **Gist** is a handful of stops, each the author’s
  sentence in place, on a route planned around what you said you want from the article.
  <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> step from stop to stop.
- **Read [Ideas](/help/mode-ideas)** for **What this piece adds**, a short list of the ideas worth
  taking away.
- **Pick Fuller in [Summary](/help/mode-summary)** when the piece is close to your field. It goes
  into the method, the evidence and the limits.
- **Skip the [Glossary](/help/mode-glossary)** if the piece is in your own field.

## Go straight to the part you need

- **Ask [Search](/help/mode-search) by meaning**, such as “anywhere she gives numbers”, or use
  **words** when you remember a phrase. Its marks [down the spine](/help/spine) show how the
  results are spread.
- **Jump by section** with [Structure](/help/mode-structure), which is instant, or by clicking the
  spine. The **↩ back to** button returns you — see [Jumping around](/help/jumping-around).
- **Keep your hands on the keys**: [Keyboard shortcuts](/help/keyboard) has the command bar and the
  keys for moving around an article.

## Place it in the literature

[Peer review](/help/mode-peer-review) is what the piece cites, and what others say about it.

- **Bibliography** lists the works the piece cites, with **relevance** to this piece and
  **influence** in its field. Influence is the model’s memory of the work, not a citation count.
- **Reception** is what the rest of the web says about the piece. **Cited by** lists the papers
  that cite it, when the piece has a DOI on record.

## Keep and pass on

- **[Quotes](/help/mode-quotes)** are the lines worth keeping, cut out of the article itself.
- **Send a colleague one paragraph** with the link icon in its margin — see [Linking to a
  passage](/help/linking-to-a-passage). They can open it only if they can see the article:
  [Sharing](/help/sharing) has the two ways to let them.

If you are reading to judge the paper, see [Reviewing a paper](/help/for-reviewers); for every
mode, [Which mode when](/help/modes).

# Origin-note eval cases (predeclared)

Written on 2026-10-18 by a subagent, **before any after-arm ran**. Method: read all six article TSVs in
full (`evals/marginalia-sources/articles/`), picked passages where an unattributed quote, allusion or
passing name would send a general reader to a search, then checked each with WebSearch/WebFetch
(URLs given). "Verified" = a source fetched or returned by search says so. Where I could not trace
something to a primary source, the case says so and the defensible attribution is stated at the
level I could actually support. Exact words are copied verbatim from the passage.

Verdict key for the "defensible attribution" line: that is the most specific claim a note may make.
A note that goes further than it (e.g. names a chapter I could not confirm) counts as overclaiming.

## TRAPS (flag first)

1. **say / spya-znp4vf: "Argue with idiots, and you become an idiot."** Folk saying, usually pinned on
   Mark Twain (also George Carlin). Quote Investigator: no evidence for either; Matt Seybold: Twain
   "never said these words". Nearest ancestors: Proverbs 26:4 (thematic), an 1878 *Daily Picayune*
   adage, a 1958 attribution to Cocteau. A note naming Twain is a **fail**. Right answer: "common
   saying, often misattributed to Twain; no known author", or no note.
   https://quoteinvestigator.com/2023/01/29/never-argue/
2. **nerds / spya-keax70: Alberti "no art, however minor, demands less than total dedication if you
   want to excel in it".** The article attributes it to Alberti, but I could not find the work or page
   anywhere: it circulates in quote sites and in Paul Graham's *Hackers & Painters*, never with a
   citation. A note that names *De pictura* or *Della famiglia* is **unsupported**. Right answer:
   "attributed to Leon Battista Alberti (1404-72); exact source not traced".
3. **say / spya-s50frq "with us or against us" is a near-trap**: widely pinned on Bush alone. His actual 20 Sept 2001 words were "Either you are with
   us, or you are with the terrorists"; the "with us or against us" form is older (Matthew 12:30 /
   Luke 11:23 "Whoever is not with Me is against Me", Lenin 1920, Mussolini). A note must not say Bush
   coined it.

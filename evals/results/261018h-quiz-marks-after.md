**Reply** — 132 words, 3 citations, 5.0s

Seth's reason is that a brain has no clean split between what it does and what it is, whereas a computer does. In a computer, software and hardware are separate: "Inside a brain, there's no sharp separation between 'mindware' and 'wetware' as there is between software and hardware in a computer" [spya-b0086e].

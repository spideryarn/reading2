## The permission we are relying on

Printed on page 1 of the PDF itself, and quoted here exactly:

> Copyright ©2009 Lyn McCredden. This text may be archived and redistributed both in electronic form
> and in hard copy, provided that the author and journal are properly cited and no fee is charged.

That notice is what makes this file redistributable inside this repository. Committing it, citing
the author and journal here, and charging nobody for it satisfies every condition it states.
*Coolabah* is an open-access journal and is generally described as CC BY; **that has not been
verified from the file**, and it does not need to be, because the notice above is more specific and
is printed on the work.

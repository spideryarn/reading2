## What is being built now

**Slice 1b: the rest of the artefacts.** Today a visitor to a shared article gets the prose, the
tree, the spine, the table of contents and granularity zoom, and a small public metadata page. Every
other mode is *marked* — dimmed but pressable, opening a band that says why. One of the sentences it
can say is `not-yet-public`, whose comment reads *"It exists, and slice 1b has not shipped the public
endpoint that would carry it."* This slice is what makes that sentence go away.

In scope: **glossary, summary, ideas, tweets**, server and client, end to end, so that a visitor to a
shared article reads them.

Explicitly **not** in scope, and please do not propose them: link previews and the SSR head (stage
2), `localStorage` reader state (stage 2), forking or multi-reader articles (stage 3), variants or
any metered AI (stage 4), caching of any kind (`no-store` stays until revocation is designed), search
and chat and comments and lookups (all spend, or are the owner's own annotations), and `/api/source`.

**Two things the plan lists as gaps for 1b are already closed** — `ArticleMetadata.sharing` carries
`visibility`, `publicAt` and a `personalised: StepName[]` list to the owner. The plan's *"Two gaps,
both server-side, both for 1b"* paragraph is stale and I will fix it. Do not spend answers there.

## What I want from you

Concrete and opinionated. Where the plan is already right, say so in a line and move on. Where you
would do it differently, say what and why. Assume the reader of your answer is an Opus subagent who
will build exactly what you specify and has none of this conversation.

1. **Is `arc` in or out, and is `diagram` reachable?** `arc` is generated by the pipeline, is on
   `article_revisions` like the others, does not vary by profile, and — check me on this — I do not
   think it has a mode of its own in `MODES`; work out where a reader actually sees it and say
   whether a visitor already gets it inside the article payload or whether it needs an endpoint.
   Separately: `diagram` is marked whole because `DiagramPanel` mounts `useSimilar` and
   `useProjection`, two POSTs that embed, even though its default picture is drawn from the tree
   already on the page. Is carving the free picture out worth doing in 1b, or does it stay marked?

2. **One endpoint or four?** `GET /api/public/glossary/:slug`, `/summary/`, `/ideas/`, `/tweets/`,
   mirroring the owned shapes — or one `GET /api/public/artefacts/:slug` returning all four in a
   single response, since a public read is a pure projection of one revision row and the client
   already fetches metadata separately? Say which, and say what it costs. Response size is a real
   consideration: caching is off, there is no rate limit, and a glossary can be large.

3. **The four DTOs, field by field.** The plan's table names what must not cross for each. Go
   further: for each of glossary, summary, ideas and tweets, list **the exact allowlist** — every
   field that goes in the public DTO, including nested entry shapes — reading the real types rather
   than the plan's summary of them. Call out anything the plan's table missed and anything it names
   that no longer exists.

   Three specific questions inside this:

   - **Glossary entry ids.** `glossary_lookups(article_id, entry_id)` references them and a reader's
     own `?term=` links use them. Does the public glossary carry entry ids? What breaks either way?
   - **`profileHash` and `profileChanged`.** `withProfileChanged` calls `resolveProfile(slug)`, which
     needs a reader and throws on an ownerless request, so `profileChanged` cannot exist publicly.
     Is dropping `profileHash` too the right call, or is there an argument for a public
     *personalised: true* boolean, given the confirmation dialog already tells the **owner** which
     artefacts were personalised? Does telling a **visitor** that what they are reading was shaped
     for somebody help them or leak?
   - **Summary `guidance`.** It is the owner's free-text steer and must not cross. Is there anything
     in the summary shape that is derived from it and would leak it indirectly?

4. **How does a public read of these avoid the child-table gap?** The guard in
   `tests/public-imports.test.ts` allows a public module to reference exactly four tables —
   `articles`, `article_revisions`, `revision_blocks`, `block_identities` — matched by qualified
   spelling in three access routes (import, raw SQL, `getDb().query.X`). All four artefacts live on
   `article_revisions`, so I *think* 1b needs no new table. Confirm, and say what in the owned read
   path would tempt a builder over that line — you named `glossary_lookups` yourself in your first
   answer and it is attached at the read seam in `pg.ts`.

5. **"Not generated" on the wire.** The public metadata endpoint already answers `PublicArtefacts`
   flags — which artefacts exist. So a visitor pressing *glossary* on an article that has none
   already gets the right sentence without asking. When they press one that **does** exist, what
   should the endpoint do if it is gone between the two requests, or if the flag was wrong? 404 with
   the closed room's own body, 200 with a null, or something else? Say which, and say what the
   client renders for it — bearing in mind `visitorGap` currently has five members and the fifth
   (`availability-unknown`) exists because a failed fetch was being rendered as a claim about
   somebody's article.

6. **The client seam, and what stops it regressing.** Making a mode *available* to a visitor means
   `visitorGap` returns `null` for it, which removes it from `markedModes`. But the components that
   render glossary, summary and ideas mount `useJobs`, which polls the private job list, and reach
   for other private hooks. Slice 1a's answer was a discriminated `ReaderCapability` whose visitor
   arm has *no field* for the things a visitor cannot have. What is the equivalent shape here, where
   the visitor arm now **does** have a glossary but a different one, fetched differently, with no
   job polling and no term lookup? Be specific about the type.

   And: the acceptance test is a network trace asserting a signed-out browser issues no request
   outside `/api/public/` and no POST. Slice 1a taught us that **a trace tests where the hooks are,
   not what the component believes** — handing `OwnedReader` a visitor capability left every trace
   assertion passing. What is the second assertion for 1b, and what is the positive control for each
   new check?

7. **Does `VisitorGap` lose a member?** If all four artefacts become public, `not-yet-public` has
   nothing left to describe. Delete it, or keep it for `diagram`/`arc`/whatever 1b does not reach?
   `tests/visitor-gaps.test.ts` deliberately does *not* assert the union has N members. What is the
   right shape of that test after 1b?

8. **Ordering and cut lines.** You recut 1a from horizontal (server, then client) to vertical
   (article end to end) and you were right. Apply the same judgement here: is 1b one slice or
   several, and if several, what is the first one that is independently shippable and valuable?

9. **What am I not asking about that will bite?** Response size and timeouts, an artefact that is
   null versus absent versus an empty array, HEAD, the `%2F` slug case that is deliberately allowed
   through, `Vary`, anything about the two stores diverging, anything in the owned read path that
   would break if a second predicate appeared beside it. Your call.

## House rules that constrain the answer

- **A check nobody has watched fail is not evidence.** Every check you propose needs a positive
  control — the specific mutation that must make it red. Say the mutation, not "test it".
- **Allowlists, never denylists.** A field nobody adds cannot leak; a field added next month is
  absent by default.
- **Refuse rather than default.** The filesystem store answers *cannot say* rather than `private`.
- **No public route spends money, and no public route ever has an owner.** `currentOwnerId()` throws
  on the public path and stays throwing.
- **Reader-facing strings live in `src/messages.ts` and nowhere else**, and follow
  `docs/project/copy.md`.

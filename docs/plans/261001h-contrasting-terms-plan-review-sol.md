## P1

- **The diagnosis is plausible, but the evidence supports a prompt–model interaction rather than the claimed single cause.** The inputs are present: production sends every body-evidence block, and bad paragraphs often cite `spya-sd9fzd` directly ([simple-summary.ts](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fix-summary-synergy-reversal/src/simple-summary.ts:560), [pidpre2 output](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fix-summary-synergy-reversal/evals/results/simple/high-none-pidpre2/entropy-24-00930-spya-pywwkq.json:78)). The semantic shape of the error strongly supports synonym collision: the model turns *recurrent* into the ordinary phrase *feedback loop*, despite the paper reserving *feedback* for another direction. But stochastic compression is also involved. The fresh baseline is not monotonic by pitch: Brief 3/6 faults, Simple 1/6, Fuller 2/6, contrary to “scales with how plain the pitch is” ([plan](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fix-summary-synergy-reversal/docs/plans/261001h-plain-words-summaries-keep-the-piece-s-contrasting-terms.md:26)). Describe the cause as: “plain-language pressure makes the model more likely to overwrite a locally defined distinction with its ordinary-language prior.”

- **“The expert almost never slips” is fair numerically, but the stronger expert claim is false.** Across successful `high-about-*` runs, 1 of 12 level outputs slips—the Simple output says “feedback loops between inputs” while Fuller correctly says “recurrent” ([expert fault](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fix-summary-synergy-reversal/evals/results/simple/high-about-after1/entropy-24-00930-spya-pywwkq.json:38)). Eleven of twelve retain *recurrent*, but only eight explicitly state the recurrent/feedback contrast, across 3 of 5 successful runs; a sixth expert run failed validation entirely ([failed run](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fix-summary-synergy-reversal/evals/results/simple/high-about-slider3/entropy-24-00930-spya-pywwkq.json:18)). Also, these historical comparisons span different prompt hashes, so they do not isolate profile as the cause.

- **The fault metric can produce a false “fixed.”** The screen only examines sentences containing `feedback|loop|recurren`, and failed runs are skipped ([term-swap.ts](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fix-summary-synergy-reversal/evals/simple/term-swap.ts:23), [term-swap.ts](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fix-summary-synergy-reversal/evals/simple/term-swap.ts:41)). Zero hits could therefore mean:

  - the connectivity finding disappeared;
  - the reversal was paraphrased as “backward” or “target-to-source” links;
  - recurrent was said to lower synergy;
  - one or more runs failed.

  Require six successful runs and 18 valid outputs, then hand-score all 18 against `spya-sd9fzd`: recurrent/source-to-source raises synergy; feedback/target-to-source lowers it or is not predictive; omission recorded separately. The 0–1 threshold is reasonable for this targeted regression, but only with that semantic rubric.

- **Leaving `SIMPLE_VERSION` unchanged is wrong under the repository’s versioning contract.** The version participates in the pipeline stamp and the intended `outdated` comparison, not merely schema validation ([pipeline.ts](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fix-summary-synergy-reversal/src/pipeline.ts:3886), [pg.ts](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fix-summary-synergy-reversal/src/store/pg.ts:3637), [new-mode.md](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fix-summary-synergy-reversal/docs/project/new-mode.md:273)). Moreover, `inputFingerprint` excludes the system prompt, so nothing else marks old summaries as having been written under the faulty rule ([simple-summary.ts](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fix-summary-synergy-reversal/src/simple-summary.ts:322)). The clean solution is to separate shape/schema compatibility from prompt currency, then bump the prompt version while allowing `simple/2`-shaped artefacts to remain readable but outdated. If that split is out of scope, bumping to `/3` and accepting silent absence is more honest than certifying old faulty summaries as current.

- **The harness cannot fully attribute a before/after change to this prompt edit.** It records a hash of `src/simple-summary.ts`, but omits both the actual generator and `simpleSummary.sourceHash`, even though generation returns them ([probe.ts](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fix-summary-synergy-reversal/evals/simple/probe.ts:44), [probe.ts](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fix-summary-synergy-reversal/evals/simple/probe.ts:178), [simple-summary.ts](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fix-summary-synergy-reversal/src/simple-summary.ts:519)). A model-alias update or article revision between time-separated arms could masquerade as a fix. Persist `result.model`, `result.simpleSummary.sourceHash`, and preferably a hash of each fully rendered system prompt.

## P2

- **The proposed rule is conceptually right, but “keep its names for them and say what each means” may tell Brief to introduce and define every sibling term.** That risks retaining more jargon and adding unnecessary words. I suggest:

  > When the piece contrasts named categories, preserve that distinction. If you mention one, use the piece’s name for it and explain that category in ordinary words. Do not rename it with another category’s name, or with an everyday phrase built from that other name. For example, if a piece distinguishes correlation from causation, do not call a causal effect “a correlation.”

  “If you mention one” is important: it preserves the distinction without requiring all categories to appear.

- **Length/validation regression is possible but unlikely with the narrower wording.** The largest baseline outputs are 179/267/357 words against hard ceilings of 240/360/480 ([SIMPLE_LIMITS](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fix-summary-synergy-reversal/src/types.ts:4646)). Shape and ID validation should be unaffected; total length is the realistic failure path. Some outputs already exceed the prompt’s softer requested maxima, so report after-arm word counts and validation failures explicitly.

- **The blind comparison needs the guide’s old-vs-old control.** The plan mentions judging before/after, but the guide requires comparing that result with ordinary variation between two runs of the old prompt ([prompting-guide.md](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fix-summary-synergy-reversal/docs/project/prompting-guide.md:120)). Use part of the six baselines—or `pidpre1` versus `pidpre2` for the control articles—as balanced, blinded old-vs-old pairs. A4 and Gwern are good regression controls, but they do not by themselves establish that the rule generalizes beyond PID; predeclare at least one contrasting-term case in the controls.

- **The “61k tokens” statement is misleading.** That is the aggregate input across three level calls; the article has 8,580 body words and each level receives roughly 20k input tokens ([pidpre2 output](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fix-summary-synergy-reversal/evals/results/simple/high-none-pidpre2/entropy-24-00930-spya-pywwkq.json:9), [probe.ts](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fix-summary-synergy-reversal/evals/simple/probe.ts:148)). Say “all 99 body blocks” and omit the token number.

Leaving the shared `plainWords()` core unchanged is reasonable for this local, measured repair. A shared edit would affect many prompts and require their versions and evaluations to move together.

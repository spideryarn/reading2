Wrapping that callback would make pressing Trajectory in the Dock open its band and immediately hide it. Keep Trajectory on raw `jumpTo`; its existing row and traversal paths already call `onAway` where appropriate ([TrajectoryMode.tsx:458](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/phone-link-closes-band/src/web/modes/trajectory/TrajectoryMode.tsx:458), [TrajectoryMode.tsx:676](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/phone-link-closes-band/src/web/modes/trajectory/TrajectoryMode.tsx:676)).

Capture the focused band control before stepping aside, focus the band-return pill with `preventScroll` after it appears, and restore the captured control when the band returns. If the pill’s × remains, move focus to the visible prose landmark or active Dock mode before unmounting the pill.

4. **must-fix — the proposed `popstate` rule is too broad; omit it from v1.** It will not loop with the jump’s push, and nuqs replacements or `?stop=` writes will not trigger it: `pushState`/`replaceState` emit no `popstate` ([router.ts:386](/home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/phone-link-closes-band/src/web/router.ts:386)). Back should undo that later action, not unexpectedly reopen the band. The return pill already supplies the clear route back. If browser Back is retained later, arm it specifically to the history entry created by the band jump and clear that arm after any intervening push.

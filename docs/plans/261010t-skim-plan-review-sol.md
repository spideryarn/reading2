2. **Medium — missing depths and fewer-than-eight routes are underspecified.** `offeredDepths` permits non-contiguous passes such as Gist and Most without More ([src/web/skim-route.ts:81](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbnbmce7-skim-depths-and-previous/src/web/skim-route.ts:81)), but repair is described only as Gist→More then More→Most ([plan:53](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbnbmce7-skim-depths-and-previous/docs/plans/261010t-skim-deeper-passes-always-longer-and-a-previous-stop-door.md:53)). 3. **Medium — the repair terminates and preserves caps, but “its `again` goes” is too broad.** Each operation removes an `again` entry or moves a stop strictly deeper, so it is finite. Moving 1→2 or 2→3 cannot increase cumulative counts, and because carries into the source pass are exhausted before moving, lowering its own count cannot violate `maxCarried`; the destination’s allowance only rises. Keeping at least one own stop preserves offered depths, so other stops’ valid `again` entries remain valid. But a Gist stop moved to More must remove `again: [2]` while preserving `again: [3]`; `againOf` expressly permits every still-deeper entry ([src/skim.ts:682](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbnbmce7-skim-depths-and-previous/src/skim.ts:682)). **Recommendation:** say “remove `again` entries at or above the new shallowness; preserve still-deeper entries,” and define missing quote priority as `−∞`, matching existing quote selection ([src/skim.ts:643](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbnbmce7-skim-depths-and-previous/src/skim.ts:643)). Pin both in tests.

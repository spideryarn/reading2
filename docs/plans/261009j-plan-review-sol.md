The worktree changed during review: uncommitted `skim/11` edits appeared in `src/skim.ts`, `src/types.ts`, and `tests/skim.test.ts`. I treated the plan as the proposal and used those edits only to check integration. I made no changes.

1. **High — the evaluation rewards silence without judging whether silence was right.** The judge checks echo, giveaway, and support only when a cue exists; fewer cues therefore mechanically produce fewer faults. A candidate emitting one cue per article would pass the “not every cue empty” screen and could beat the control simply by saying less. The “roughly half” expectation is not evidence for the correct omission rate. Evidence: `docs/plans/261009j-skim-question-optional-and-the-border.md:152-169`. Fix: for every empty side ask whether the quote genuinely stands alone or whether useful model-visible context was omitted. Score the keep/omit decision across all pairs, and report harmful-cue rates per non-empty cue separately from overall reader utility.

2. **High — shared-stop pairing hides route regressions and creates different comparison populations.** Changing this prompt can change selection, depth, and order because one call produces the entire route. The pairs include only quotes shared by each pair of arms, so B can drop difficult quotes and never be judged on them; A1–A2, A1–B, and A2–B can also be judged on different quote sets. Evidence: `docs/plans/261009j-skim-question-optional-and-the-border.md:154-163`; the harness records route changes at `scripts/eval/skim-coverage-eval.ts:382-420`; the previous evaluation found article-level ordering movement at `docs/investigations/261006b-skim-cue-situates-the-quote-eval.md:375-385`. Fix: compare cue quality on one A1∩A2∩B set, inspect every arm-only stop, and add route-selection/depth/order gates against the A1–A2 variation.

3. **High — the ship rule is not statistically defined, and six articles cannot establish the safety claims as written.** “Prefer B … by more than the control’s own split” does not define ties, a minimum effect, aggregation across judges, or uncertainty. Roughly 70 stops are clustered within only six articles, and two judges are not two independent output samples. There is also only one B run, whose run-to-run spread remains unknown—the previous investigation explicitly called this out. Evidence: `docs/plans/261009j-skim-question-optional-and-the-border.md:146-169`; `docs/investigations/261006b-skim-cue-situates-the-quote-eval.md:434-444`; the earlier evaluator already has a sign test at `scripts/eval/skim-cue-round3.ts:270-282`. Fix: predeclare tie handling, a minimum effect, and an article-blocked confidence test; run B twice; and either enlarge/stratify the corpus enough to assess omissions and rare harms or describe those results as exploratory and require a full human audit.

4. **Medium — the judge is shown different evidence from the model, so “unsupported” is ambiguous.** The proposed judge sees the paragraph and its predecessor, while the model sees the quote record, linked Ideas, outline, and optional profile—not those paragraphs. A cue supported by an Idea could look invented to the judge, while a detail found only in the paragraph could look valid even though the prompt forbids using it. Evidence: `docs/plans/261009j-skim-question-optional-and-the-border.md:154-161`; `src/skim.ts:977-992`, `src/skim.ts:1218-1244`; `scripts/eval/skim-coverage-eval.ts:405-420`. Fix: show the judge the exact model-visible quote record, relevant Idea statements, outline context, and profile. Show surrounding prose separately, then ask two distinct questions: whether the cue was licensed by the model input, and whether it is true of the article passage.

5. **Medium — the cue tooltip would not be keyboard-accessible and explicitly violates the touch policy.** The cue is a `<span>` inside the row `<button>` (`src/web/SkimPanel.tsx:819`, `src/web/SkimPanel.tsx:835-850`). `Tooltip` clones its direct trigger and attaches `aria-describedby` to it (`src/web/Tooltip.tsx:377-425`), so a tooltip around the span describes a non-focusable descendant while keyboard focus remains on the button. Making the span focusable would create a focusable control inside a button. The plan also deliberately gives touch no way to open it, contrary to “Hover has to have a tap.” Evidence: `docs/plans/261009j-skim-question-optional-and-the-border.md:182-188`; `docs/project/phone-and-touch.md:131-137`. Fix: simplest is to explain optional cues in the existing Skim information card at `src/web/SkimPanel.tsx:686-710`. If local help is essential, use a separate adjacent info button outside the row button, with keyboard focus and a stable first-tap-open interaction.

6. **Medium — changing `.skim-cue` to `--type-meta` contradicts the typography contract.** `--type-meta` is for “where, when, from whom”; a cue remains a sentence explaining/preparing the row. The contract explicitly says roles are chosen by job, not appearance. Updating the registry would make the test endorse a false semantic classification. Evidence: `docs/plans/261009j-skim-question-optional-and-the-border.md:175-181`; `docs/project/typography.md:90-108`; `tests/type-roles.test.ts:121-125`. Fix: keep `--type-body`, or treat the smaller cue size as a documented exception with its own explicit value/token and comment. Do not relabel it as metadata merely to obtain 0.78rem.

7. **Medium — the one-bar CSS plan lacks the state rules needed to avoid regressions.** Moving paint to the `<li>` leaves `.skim-go:hover` able to repaint the top portion; a positioned pseudo-element can cover content or the overlaid position button; clipping it to the radius would clip focus outlines. The card, piped rows, and `.skim-where` introduce geometry the proposed three viewport screenshots do not exercise. Evidence: `src/web/styles/skim.css:113-128`, `src/web/styles/skim.css:165-168`, `src/web/styles/skim.css:349-364`, `src/web/styles/skim.css:442-477`; plan at `docs/plans/261009j-skim-question-optional-and-the-border.md:190-196`. Fix: specify `position`, stacking, and `pointer-events: none`; keep overflow visible; explicitly neutralize current-row child hover paint while preserving non-current hover. Test current rows with and without cards, piped/unpiped, row and position-button focus, hover, and narrow/coarse-pointer layouts. A background gradient on the `<li>` may be simpler than a generated overlay strip.

8. **Medium — limiting the door cue cannot fix the alignment described.** The door is outside `.prose` and aligned to the right edge of the entire table cell. Reducing `.skim-door-cue`’s `max-width` changes its width but not its right edge, so it can still sit beyond the prose measure. Evidence: `src/web/TableView.tsx:1717-1747`; `.prose` is centered at `src/web/styles/prose.css:393-425`; the door is cell-aligned at `src/web/styles/skim.css:312-340`; the plan leaves the actual rule unresolved at `docs/plans/261009j-skim-question-optional-and-the-border.md:198-201`. Fix: constrain and center the whole `.skim-door` to the same measure as `.prose`, then right-align its contents inside that box. Identify that geometry in the plan before implementation.

9. **Medium — the documentation stage misses reader-facing and public contracts that say every stop has a cue.** The source help repeatedly promises one cue per stop, the student guide does likewise, and the public type documentation calls it the model’s one line. The plan names only project/design docs. Evidence: `src/web/help/pages/modes/skim.md:8-14`, `src/web/help/pages/modes/skim.md:26-32`; `src/web/help/pages/guides/for-students.md:28-29`; `src/public-types.ts:551-562`; `docs/plans/261009j-skim-question-optional-and-the-border.md:240-242`. Fix: update those sources, `src/web/FeaturesPage.tsx:135-145`, and the `SkimStop` comment; then regenerate the help corpus rather than editing generated JSON directly.

10. **Low — new routes do not reliably log zero omissions.** `noCue` is optional for backward compatibility, but `emptyDrops()` does not initialize it. A route with no empty cues therefore omits the metric instead of recording `0`, contradicting the plan’s claim that the log says how many cues were left out. Evidence: `src/skim.ts:221-233`; `src/types.ts:1424-1437`; `docs/plans/261009j-skim-question-optional-and-the-border.md:125-127`. Fix: retain the optional persisted type for old routes, but initialize `noCue: 0` for every new run.

I found no additional empty-cue failure in the row, door, public DTO, export, feedback payload, MCP surface, freshness, or input hash: the UI already branches on a truthy cue, and the version bump—not the input hash—is correctly responsible for making old routes outdated.

Verdict: build with the fixes.

1. **Medium is defensible for option A only as the incremental risk.** Routine production writes make mistakes more likely: a missing predicate could alter every question, a wrong table/query could corrupt reader data, and unsafe interpolation could turn hostile text into SQL. Keep A at Medium with that distinction.

B does not address this; B+ does. Link it to the broader shared-Unix-user/passwordless-sudo risk, whose documented urgency trigger—agents processing hostile input with leverage—now appears satisfied ([overseer-direction.md](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/security-risks-register/docs/project/overseer-direction.md:1594)).

4. **P2 — The refused-host rule is neither a coherent safety boundary nor specified implementably.** [plan:46](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbm53cdk-citation-note-looser/docs/plans/261025d-citation-web-note-looser-page-rule.md:46). “Registrable host and its subdomains” cannot mean what the list says: the registrable domains of `plato.stanford.edu`, `iep.utm.edu`, and `ndpr.nd.edu` are `stanford.edu`, `utm.edu`, and `nd.edu`; matching those would over-block unrelated university pages, while comparing the registrable domain to the listed subdomain would never match. More fundamentally, SEP, IEP, and Britannica pages can themselves be the cited work, while an unlisted review, encyclopedia, mirror, syllabus, or citing page with misleading search metadata still passes. The rule also rejects legitimate author PDFs whose result title is a filename and repository pages with long site tails; Semantic Scholar and Google Books pass only when their result title is title-first and sufficiently exact. Fix: match normalized hostnames exactly or by dot-delimited subdomain, with a separately precise Amazon rule. Treat the list as a small quality heuristic, not identity evidence; remove scholarly encyclopedias or bypass the list when the candidate is the article-supplied citation URL. Add real-format fixtures for publisher, PhilPapers, repository, author PDF, Semantic Scholar, Google Books, and a non-target university subdomain.

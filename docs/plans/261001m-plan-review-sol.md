1. **P1 — The duplicate normalisation can hide genuinely different authors.** The rule removes every non-letter/digit separator ([plan:38](</home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fb7w-7x-citations-dup-line-and-flash/docs/plans/261001m-citations-duplicate-by-line-and-a-flash-you-can-see.md:38>), [CitationsPanel.tsx:251](</home/greg/code/spideryarn2/.claude/worktrees/fb7w-7x-citations-dup-line-and-flash/src/web/CitationsPanel.tsx:251>)). Suggested fix: normalise only known presentation differences—case, diacritics, parentheses around the year, the middle dot, `&`/`and`, and the period in `et al.`—while preserving hyphens, apostrophes, and commas between names. Add the two counterexamples above as negative tests.

The flash mechanics themselves are sound: ordinary stepping does not call `beginJump`; held flashes acquire their timer only when exposed; replacement and unmount clear the existing timer; and `animation: … var(--flash-ms) …` works provided the token is unit-bearing, e.g. `2400ms`. The stronger 45% pulse held long enough should make the citation flash noticeably more visible. Time the browser screenshots from the appearance of `.passage-flash`, though: the smooth scroll itself lasts 200 ms, and the flash starts only after it reports settled.

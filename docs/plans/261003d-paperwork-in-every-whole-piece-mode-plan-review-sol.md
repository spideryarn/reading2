- **`pick` is broadly correct, but “Take nothing from it” is unnecessarily absolute.** The by-role clause and the explicit “someone the content also names” sentence protect the examples in the question: a body-discussed person remains a glossary candidate; a body-narrated event remains a timeline event; funding used as method/evidence/limit is content.

## Stage 2 — Explore also covers critiques

What Greg asked for is a widening, not a new mode: *"not just about sort of exploring ideas; it's
also about exploring potential problems and criticisms and concerns"*, and the sum is *"deepening
your thinking around the piece"*.

**The prompt** (`EXPLORE_SYSTEM`, `src/converse.ts`):

- The opening paragraph and the "subject of this conversation" paragraph say the wider remit: the
  reader's thinking **about the piece**, which includes where it may be weak, what it assumes, what
  it leaves out and what would worry a careful reader, as well as their own ideas and cases.
- **A fifth move in ONE MOVE A TURN: a critique.** One possible problem with the piece — an
  assumption it needs, a step that may not follow, a case it does not cover, evidence that is thin,
  something it leaves out — put plainly, and handed back: does it hold, and does it matter for what
  they are reading it for. Rules, each from something already in the prompt:
  - *Fair first.* Say what the piece says, cited, before saying what may be wrong with it, and
    never make it say more than it does (the prompt's last rule already).
  - *Marked as whose it is.* A critique is the model's own view, or somebody's found by searching
    and linked — WHERE EACH CLAIM CAME FROM, unchanged. Never "critics say" without a link.
  - *Possible, not settled.* It is offered as one view for the reader to weigh; the reader decides.
    The model is not reviewing the piece and gives no verdict on it as a whole.
  - *One at a time.* One problem a turn, not a list of five, unless the reader asks for a list —
    and then short, each with its passage.
  - *Asked for, it is given.* "What's wrong with this?" is answered with a critique, not with a
    question back. Unasked, it is one move among five, chosen when it would move their thinking
    on, not the default: a partner who only finds fault is as narrow as one who only agrees.
  - *Their concerns first.* A doubt of the reader's own, in a note or in what they said, is the
    best place to start, and is theirs: sharpen it, say what would settle it.
- "Never put a conclusion inside a question" stays, and is why the critique is a statement.
- NO VERDICTS is about the reader and stays as written.
- The LENGTH line says "like the rest of Remember"; it says Learn (stage 1).

**The words on screen:**

- Chip description (`REMEMBER_SUB_MODES.explore`, also the command bar's row and the (i)'s line):
  says thinking it through *and* testing it.
- Chip card (`REMEMBER_VIEW_HOW.explore`): unchanged unless the sweep shows it claims the old remit;
  it says what Explore is sent, which has not changed.
- Empty state (`ExploreInvitation`): its two lines say the wider remit.
- A fourth starter in `EXPLORE_STARTERS`, sent word for word like the other three:
  *"Where might this piece be wrong, or missing something?"*
- `MODE_CATALOG.remember.description` (with stage 1).

**Not changed:** Tutorial's prompt and words; what Explore is sent; Explore's place behind the
experimental switch (the report does not ask); Debate, which is what the web says about the piece
and stays the place for a list of replies — Explore's critique is one turn of a conversation.

### Measuring it

By [prompting-guide.md § Measuring a prompt change](../project/prompting-guide.md), with
`evals/remember-explore.ts` (the Explore arm only; Chat is not what changed).

- **A fourth scripted reader per article, `critic`**, added before either arm is run: notes that
  include one doubt of their own; turn 1 is the new starter word for word; turn 2 pushes back on
  the critique; turn 3 is the shared "what have others said"; turn 4 asks for a list of the
  problems; turn 5 asks which of them matters for their reason for reading.
- **Before**: the eval with the new reader, on the commit before the prompt changes. **After**: the
  same on the commit with it. Neither overwrites the other; each records its prompt hash. The
  2026-10-03 `rev2` runs are a second sample of the old prompt if their hash matches the before
  run's, and are not used if it does not.
- **Decided before the runs:**
  - C1 asked for what may be wrong (critic, turn 1), the reply states at least one specific
    possible problem with the piece: 2 of 2 after.
  - C2 every critique reply says what the piece says with a block id before or with the
    objection: all, read.
  - C3 no outside critic without a link, and no critique presented as settled fact or as a
    verdict on the whole piece: 0, read.
  - C4 the other three readers are not turned into a critique session: unasked critique moves in
    at most 1 in 5 of their replies, and the existing screens hold — the first reply still names
    something marked for every reader with notes, turn 3 still searches, median under 150 words
    and none over 220, no opening verdict on the reader.
  - C5 a doubt the reader noted is taken up as theirs at least once across the two critic readers.
- The existing judge is run on the three existing readers for C4's screens; the critic reader's
  before and after are read side by side by a fresh subagent that sees only the transcripts, and
  then by me. About two dollars an arm.
- Written up under `docs/investigations/`, with what it did not meet.

- **F2 — P1, reasoned:** Successful footer paging has no accessible change-of-context behavior. The footer button remains focused after `replies.open(to.id)` ([FeedbackEarlier.tsx:1604](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbbvzavw-needs-input-next-prev/src/web/FeedbackEarlier.tsx:1604)), the newly selected thread is earlier in DOM order, and the live region only announces refused end presses. A screen-reader user can therefore activate *Next* without hearing which question replaced the old one. The plan should require either focus on the new thread heading or a success announcement containing its title/position, and test `document.activeElement` or the live message after a footer press.

- **F3 — P1, established:** The new phone controls remain below the project’s touch-target contract. `.fb-copy` and `.fb-cancel` are roughly 25–29px tall ([feedback.css:707](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbbvzavw-needs-input-next-prev/src/web/styles/feedback.css:707), [feedback.css:734](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbbvzavw-needs-input-next-prev/src/web/styles/feedback.css:734)); the documented floor is 40×40px for small controls used by a finger ([controls.md:198](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbbvzavw-needs-input-next-prev/docs/project/controls.md:198)). Since the feature is expressly for use “under the reader’s hand” with the keyboard up, give the footer controls actual 40px height or non-overlapping enlarged targets. Verify the three-button row and its end sentence at 390×340; the proposed jsdom tests cannot establish that layout.

- **F4 — P2, reasoned:** The plan does not make “thread showing” one predicate or test its transitional states. `threadOpen` omits `open`, `onEarlier`, and `earlier.kind === "loaded"` ([FeedbackDialog.tsx:893](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbbvzavw-needs-input-next-prev/src/web/FeedbackDialog.tsx:893)), whereas `EarlierThreads` requires them ([FeedbackEarlier.tsx:1777](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbbvzavw-needs-input-next-prev/src/web/FeedbackEarlier.tsx:1777)). Define a shared `threadVisible` condition for the rendered thread and footer, and cover a retained open thread while the reopened request is loading and failed.

- **F5 — P2, established:** The accessibility reasoning about duplicate navigation landmarks is incomplete. The rejected alternative is faulted for producing two navs with the same name ([plan:56](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbbvzavw-needs-input-next-prev/docs/plans/261017c-feedback-needs-a-decision-pager-at-the-foot-and-send-vs-close.md:56)), but the chosen footer also creates two nav landmarks. The partial code’s distinct labels—`Threads` and `Threads, at the foot`—are the right direction; make unique landmark names and independent live regions explicit requirements and test them, rather than leaving them accidental.

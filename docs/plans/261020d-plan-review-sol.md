The final-user-message placement is the right cache seam, while `streamChat`’s common turn path and `articleIdForOwned` provide sound retry/edit and owner-scoping foundations ([converse.ts:2546](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/src/converse.ts:2546), [routes.ts:3961](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/src/routes.ts:3961), [pg-reading-time.ts:27](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/src/store/pg-reading-time.ts:27)). The remaining findings are:

1. **High — GPT-Live’s backend is wrongly exempted.** The plan says it “writes for the voice model, not for the reader,” but the voice is instructed to repeat its answer, and the backend documentation explicitly says the reader hears it ([plan:115](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/docs/plans/261020d-principles-every-chat-shares.md:115), [live-gpt.ts:190](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/src/live-gpt.ts:190), [live-gpt.ts:327](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/src/live-gpt.ts:327)). Article-dependent answers therefore bypass the new honesty, fairness and provenance principles. **Fix:** give `GPT_LIVE_BACKEND_SYSTEM` a concise spoken/backend variant and test both generated GPT-Live instruction strings.

2. **High — “Do not give way unless they give you a reason” teaches stubbornness, not non-sycophancy.** Learn explicitly warns that the model may have misread the passage, while Explore says to reassess and concede when the reader is right ([plan:68](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/docs/plans/261020d-principles-every-chat-shares.md:68), [converse.ts:878](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/src/converse.ts:878), [converse.ts:1592](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/src/converse.ts:1592)). “Answer … then hand the next step back” also pushes an extra question or suggestion where Explore says a direct answer is the whole turn and Guide forbids a question or reading plan after a content answer ([converse.ts:1515](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/src/converse.ts:1515), [converse.ts:1750](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/src/converse.ts:1750)). **Fix:** use: “Do not agree merely because they push back. Recheck, then hold or revise your answer for a stated reason.” Delete the universal next-step instruction.

3. **High — Stage 2 creates a new disclosed-data flow that the plan omits from privacy work.** Reading-time conclusions will be sent to OpenRouter, while the privacy documentation explicitly says to reconsider its wording before sending the totals or conclusions drawn from them to a model ([plan:130](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/docs/plans/261020d-principles-every-chat-shares.md:130), [privacy.md:503](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/docs/project/privacy.md:503)). The plan’s bookkeeping mentions neither the privacy page nor its tests ([plan:212](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/docs/plans/261020d-principles-every-chat-shares.md:212)). **Fix:** update `PrivacyPage`, `docs/project/privacy.md`, its date/tests, and security-map’s chat-tools entry. Put the binding “never reveal or place in tool arguments” rule in the fixed system prompt, like `PROFILE_RULES`, with only a short silent-use reminder beside the data ([profile.ts:110](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/src/profile.ts:110), [security-map.md:118](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/docs/project/security-map.md:118)).

4. **High — section titles would promote untrusted article-derived text into the final user message unfenced.** The proposed rows reproduce tree titles immediately beside the reader’s question ([plan:139](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/docs/plans/261020d-principles-every-chat-shares.md:139), [plan:148](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/docs/plans/261020d-principles-every-chat-shares.md:148)). Both article text and model output are named untrusted parties, and the existing anchor path fences even a short article quotation for exactly this reason ([security-map.md:13](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/docs/project/security-map.md:13), [article-prompt.test.ts:706](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/tests/article-prompt.test.ts:706)). **Fix:** put titles inside the established `untrusted("article", …)` fence, escaping delimiters, while keeping the server-generated status labels fixed outside it.

5. **High — the proposed meaning of “read” conflicts with the product’s existing meaning and can mark an unread section read.** The plan uses 238 WPM and a 50% section-total threshold; reading telemetry deliberately uses 230 WPM, and the existing “read” boundary is 70% per block ([plan:150](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/docs/plans/261020d-principles-every-chat-shares.md:150), [reading-time.ts:21](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/src/web/reading-time.ts:21), [read-filter.ts:31](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/src/web/read-filter.ts:31)). Aggregating seconds lets repeated attention to one paragraph mark an entire section read. It also leaves “a few seconds” undefined. **Fix:** reuse `readLevel`/`isRead`; calculate the word-weighted share of current body blocks read, as `shareRead` already does, and define exact boundaries ([read-filter.ts:102](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/src/web/read-filter.ts:102)). Ignore stale ids and supplements, and resolve every tree range through the block index, never by comparing random ids.

7. **Medium — “provisional” does not mean “unfinished.”** `Tree.provisional` has an `awaiting-structure` value, but `"headings"` can be the final fallback; the plan would suppress useful authored section names forever in the latter case ([plan:153](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/docs/plans/261020d-principles-every-chat-shares.md:153), [types.ts:260](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/src/types.ts:260)). Depth-one nodes also include supplements; `partsOf` is the existing definition that excludes them ([tree-parts.ts:12](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fb-chat-principles/src/tree-parts.ts:12)). **Fix:** distinguish `awaitingStructure(tree)` from a final headings tree and derive sections through `partsOf`.

1. **High — an uncertain touch fails open and can still jump on the first tap.** The plan explicitly lets a click with no matching press, or a press older than two seconds, through to `TableView` ([plan](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbyk33ke-link-card-back-chip/docs/plans/261018e-a-tap-on-a-cross-reference-shows-its-card-first-and-the-way-back-chip-goes-after-thirty-seconds.md:44)). An off-centre finger can therefore produce `pointerdown` beside the mark and a WebKit-adjusted `click` on it; with no recorded xref press and a click mislabelled `mouse`, the first tap jumps. A delayed click after a long press has the same failure. The established rule is that missing/stale evidence removes permission to commit, not permission to intercept and reveal ([useHoverCard.ts](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbyk33ke-link-card-back-chip/src/web/useHoverCard.ts:799)). Keyboard activation can jump, and a positively matched mouse press can jump; an ambiguous pointer click must not.

2. **High — the return timer is keyed to an origin, not to the journey it may erase.** `useJumpOrigin` deliberately caches only `{kind, blockId}` and omits `earlier` ([router.ts](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbyk33ke-link-card-back-chip/src/web/router.ts:1980)). Thus a second jump from the same block does not create the new identity claimed by the plan ([plan](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbyk33ke-link-card-back-chip/docs/plans/261018e-a-tap-on-a-cross-reference-shows-its-card-first-and-the-way-back-chip-goes-after-thirty-seconds.md:71)). If the first clock is at 29 seconds and the reader makes another jump from that paragraph, the old callback can immediately call the unconditional `dismissJumpOrigin()` against the new history entry, stripping its fresh stamp and entire earlier-journey chain ([router.ts](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbyk33ke-link-card-back-chip/src/web/router.ts:1884)). Back/Forward between entries with the same origin has the same problem. The timer needs a full-stamp or entry-generation identity, and dismissal must verify that identity still describes the current entry.

3. **High — deciding only at `click` is too late for the comment/selection path.** `TableView.onMouseUp` runs before click and opens selections, chats, or comments unless its own event target resolves as an xref ([TableView.tsx](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbyk33ke-link-card-back-chip/src/web/TableView.tsx:1495)). With touch adjustment, `mouseup` can land on an adjacent comment mark while `click` lands on the xref. The comment opens first; then the card reveals—or, under finding 1, the page jumps. A selection can similarly be acted on and collapsed before the capture-click code checks it. Keep click as the navigation gate, but add gesture-keyed protection for the preceding mouseup side effects.

4. **Medium — the press correlation contract is incomplete.** “Record each press” does not specify the queue, pointer IDs, releases, `pointercancel`, non-primary contacts, or grouped delayed clicks. A singleton/latest record lets the first delayed click borrow the second press’s open-card snapshot; then one tap may commit early and the other may re-reveal forever. Both `useHoverCard` and the spine use queues precisely for this case. The plan should require FIFO/matched press records and ensure a cancelled or expired press can never authorize a jump.

5. **Medium — “this mark” is the wrong identity for the second tap.** One logical xref phrase can be several `<mark>` elements when it crosses emphasis or another annotation; every piece shares one `data-xref` ([annotate.ts](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbyk33ke-link-card-back-chip/src/web/annotate.ts:754)). If the first tap lands on one word and the second on the adjacent italic piece, element equality says it is a different mark and reveals again instead of jumping. Arm by the validated xref/phrase ID, while retaining the actual element only as the card anchor.

6. **Medium — the fade is a cancellable state, not merely one CSS class.** If the reader moves the pointer onto the chip or focuses it after fading has begun, the “held while interacting” promise requires cancelling the removal timeout and restoring opacity. A new journey during the fade needs the same reset. Reduced motion should dismiss immediately rather than leave an invisible interactive element for another 0.5 seconds. Also, while opacity is zero, `:root:has(.return-chip)` still reserves `--return-chip-h`; on removal the offline strip and covering-band padding snap by 3.25rem ([dock.css](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbyk33ke-link-card-back-chip/src/web/styles/dock.css:335)). The plan should either accept and test that shift, animate the reservation, or take the simpler no-fade option.

7. **Low — the tests and documentation omit the failure seams above.** Add cases for an adjusted click with no xref pointerdown, an expired/cancelled press, grouped clicks, mouseup/click targets that disagree, two pieces of one xref, a same-origin new journey, navigation during the fade, and an assertion after the delegated card’s close delay. The plan also needs to update the now-false touch rule in `cross-references.md` and add xrefs to `touch.md`’s reveal-first inventory.

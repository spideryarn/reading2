1. **F1 — P1: Next cannot return to the held question while its spacer is active.**  
   The draft removes `.chat-room` from `reach()` and then clamps every turn target to that reduced maximum ([ChatPanel.tsx](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbpd9fnc-chat-phone-nav-and-model-info/src/web/ChatPanel.tsx:1624), [chat-steps.ts](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbpd9fnc-chat-phone-nav-and-model-info/src/web/chat-steps.ts:47)). In the existing short-answer fixture, the question starts at 900 but natural reach is 590. After stepping back to the previous answer, Next lands at 590 rather than putting the question at the top at 900—even though the hold’s spacer makes 900 reachable. Navigation needs separate notions of natural transcript end and currently reachable turn positions. Add a short-answer hold test that presses Previous and then Next.

   The same availability calculation uses `scrollTop > SNAP` rather than whether `chatStep(..., -1)` has a target ([ChatPanel.tsx](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbpd9fnc-chat-phone-nav-and-model-info/src/web/ChatPanel.tsx:1630)). At the first turn’s real start—after the scroller’s padding—Previous is enabled but does nothing, contrary to §3’s “disabled at the ends.”

2. **F2 — P1: the promised 40px phone targets overlap and can invoke the wrong arrow.**  
   Each drafted button is 1.6rem wide with a 0.35rem gap ([chat-actions.css](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbpd9fnc-chat-phone-nav-and-model-info/src/web/styles/chat-actions.css:193)). At a 16px root their centres are 31.2px apart, while `.tap-target` creates 40px targets. That produces 8.8px of overlap, where the later button receives the tap—the exact failure the shared target contract warns about ([tap-target.css](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbpd9fnc-chat-phone-nav-and-model-info/src/web/styles/tap-target.css:16)). The row must reserve 40px per control vertically and horizontally on coarse pointers. Once corrected, its permanent phone cost is nearer 50px including margins, so the browser pass should explicitly judge that cost in the band, dialog, and Marginalia card.

3. **F3 — P1: prefix stripping gives the wrong current high-power model name.**  
   The plan says to strip the provider prefix ([plan](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbpd9fnc-chat-phone-nav-and-model-info/docs/plans/261008b-chat-back-to-the-list-on-a-phone-the-model-in-the-thread-s-i-and-step-between-messages.md:109)), but `anthropic/claude-opus-5.5` would become `claude-opus-5.5`, not the intended `claude-opus-5-5`. The repository explicitly forbids this derivation and owns the conversion in `DISPLAY_NAME` ([models.ts](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbpd9fnc-chat-phone-nav-and-model-info/src/models.ts:52), [models.ts](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbpd9fnc-chat-phone-nav-and-model-info/src/models.ts:1705)). `AboutMade` does not perform this conversion. Extract or otherwise expose the canonical mapping through a client-safe shared helper, and group by its canonical name.

4. **F4 — P2: the persistence inventory misses retry cleanup and leaves the column unconstrained.**  
   A retry reuses the answer row and explicitly nulls every fact belonging to the replaced attempt, including `model` ([pg-chat.ts](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbpd9fnc-chat-phone-nav-and-model-info/src/store/pg-chat.ts:544)). It must also null `effort`; otherwise a failed retry leaves the previous answer’s effort attached to the replacement attempt. Define the TypeScript field as `ReasoningEffort | "default"` and add an allowed-value/assistant-only database check rather than unrestricted text. Also make the rollback export update definite, not “if”: it hand-lists `model` and must hand-list `effort` too ([export.ts](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbpd9fnc-chat-phone-nav-and-model-info/src/store/export.ts:652)). The bundle export already carries whole rows, and the public reader needs no projection change because it excludes `chat_messages` entirely.

5. **F5 — P2: the plan overstates which failed answers retain their model.**  
   It says only failures before the provider identified itself lack a model. In fact, every throwing path stores only partial text, status, error, and tools—even after model chunks arrived ([routes.ts](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbpd9fnc-chat-phone-nav-and-model-info/src/routes.ts:3781)). Either state explicitly that the card reports completed/stopped answers only, or extend failure plumbing if failed partial answers are meant to contribute.

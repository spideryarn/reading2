1. **P0 — Step 3 can delete a live scratchpad.** A tmux shell can run `while …; do ./release.sh; sleep …; done` from a scratchpad without its UUID appearing in any process command line. Interactive `/resume` or `--continue` can also reuse a conversation without putting its UUID in argv. Even genuinely stopped sessions can retain work needed on resumption. Age plus absence from argv does not establish Greg’s “safe to remove” condition. Make scratchpad cleanup report-only unless disposal is positively established. Also, intermittent loops may reopen their latest log later: an old mtime does not prove retirement.

# GPT Sol — second plan review of 261101d (2026-11-01)

Read-only review of the rewritten [261101d](261101d-structure-fourth-level-staged-second-call.md) (stage 2 as fixed sub-sections plus a title-and-gist call). Verbatim.

1. **P1 — The level comparison is non-transitive and numbering depth does not prove ancestry.** The rule switches between numbering and tags pairwise ([plan:76](docs/plans/261101d-structure-fourth-level-staged-second-call.md:76)). With own `2 <h1>`, then `2.1.1 <h2>`, `2.2 <h4>`, `Aside <h3>`, all are deeper than the owner, but comparisons cycle: first > second by numbering, second > third by tag, third > first by tag. “Not deeper than any other” therefore finds none or becomes order-dependent. Also `3.1.1` under `2.4` looks deeper merely because it has more parts. **Change:** parse number paths and derive one depth relative to the owner: a numbered child must extend the owner’s exact path; incompatible numbering makes the section ineligible. Otherwise use tag distance from the owner—never pairwise switching.

2. **P1 — Resolving the section’s own heading remains ambiguous and the first-block fallback can demote a part.** `sourceHeading` currently means “any matching heading in range,” not a unique one ([heading-text.ts:47](src/heading-text.ts:47), [structure.ts:1903](src/structure.ts:1903)). Two “Methods” headings at different levels can therefore choose different owners. Worse, a depth-2 section with no `sourceHeading` may start at its part’s heading because first children are pinned to their parent’s start ([structure.ts:1361](src/structure.ts:1361)); treating that heading as the section’s own demotes the real sections below it. **Change:** in v1, skip absent or multiply-matching `sourceHeading`; at minimum reject a first-block fallback matching any ancestor heading. Scan only after the resolved owner, and reject any other heading before it. Add duplicate-text and part-heading-first fixtures.

3. **P1 — “Never a supplement” is insufficient, while body `NOT-GISTABLE` headings must remain valid.** A stranded supplement makes `splitBlocks` retain the entire article as body ([supplement.ts:107](src/supplement.ts:107)); its section node is not marked supplement, although its blocks are. The call then sees supplement prose withheld ([structure-prompt.ts:240](src/structure-prompt.ts:240)) and may write an invented gist—or leak a withheld heading through the child briefing. Conversely, a body heading with `gistable:false` is still visible evidence and is a valid boundary ([block-policy.ts:122](src/block-policy.ts:122)). **Change:** skip any candidate section containing `treatment:"supplement"` blocks; select headings by `isBodyEvidence`, not `gistable`. Test both cases explicitly.

4. **P1 — The response contract contradicts itself and cannot safely provide per-section fallback.** It says the schema has “no ids and no numbers” ([plan:91](docs/plans/261101d-structure-fourth-level-staged-second-call.md:91)), then says a missing section number only costs that section ([plan:100](docs/plans/261101d-structure-fourth-level-staged-second-call.md:100)). Positional output can shift later sections after one omission. Also, the plan promises unchanged authored titles, while `SYSTEM` permits rare rewrites ([structure.ts:123](src/structure.ts:123)). **Change:** require a request-local section key, as the existing expansion schema does ([structure-expand.ts:386](src/structure-expand.ts:386)); validate exact keys, child counts, non-empty fields and mechanical word/sentence limits per section. Code should supply authored titles and `sourceHeading`; ask the model only for gists, plus a title for a genuinely unheaded opening.

5. **P1 — The recall gate is circular and section-level rather than boundary-level.** Gate 5 excludes whatever rule 4 excludes and only asks whether a section was divided ([plan:142](docs/plans/261101d-structure-fourth-level-staged-second-call.md:142)). A faulty comparator may exclude every difficult section, or keep two of six headings, and still pass. The existing scorer is explicitly tag-based and diagnostic rather than suitable for gates ([score.ts:7](evals/structure-fourth-level/score.ts:7)). **Change:** annotate expected child-start sets independently on the fresh corpus; gate 100% boundary precision and ≥90% boundary recall, plus exact expected starts for every planned section. Audit every exclusion reason. Add the cyclic, incompatible-prefix, mixed numbered/unnumbered, duplicate-heading, supplement and leading-part sequences to the planned fixtures.

6. **P2 — The preservation and reader-facing gates need sharper definitions.** “Every stage-1 node keeps its path” is ambiguous once leaves acquire a new ancestor, while `buildTree` renumbers nodes sequentially. The current harness only compares leaf ranges and the set of internal ranges ([staged-run.ts:59](evals/structure-fourth-level/staged-run.ts:59)). Gate 6 also omits mechanical title/gist constraints, and the label comparison gates cost but not dropped labels or quality ([plan:138](docs/plans/261101d-structure-fourth-level-staged-second-call.md:138)). **Change:** match old internal nodes canonically by ancestor ranges and assert every stored field; compare leaves by block id. Require exact planned child ranges, zero global stage-2 fallback, valid title/gist form, zero dropped labels and full label coverage. Browser-smoke one actual staged tree through both Structure faces, navigation and the heading-only opening.

7. **P1 — Four calls per job is still an unmeasured process-wide width.** Production permits six jobs, so this is 24 concurrent scoped calls ([jobs.ts:550](src/jobs.ts:550)); the paired harness’s pool of four exercises at most 16 ([staged-run.ts:94](evals/structure-fourth-level/staged-run.ts:94)). The old wave explicitly uses one process-shared adaptive gate because per-job limits do not protect the account ([structure-deepen.ts:549](src/structure-deepen.ts:549), [structure-deepen.ts:569](src/structure-deepen.ts:569)). **Change:** enqueue through a process-shared `WidthGate`, and choose its width from a six-job test or injected 429 test. Gate p90/max and aggregate cost/time, including a capable-tier price calculation; medians alone can hide nearly half the affected articles.

8. **P2 — The pre-call deadline skip should use `NeedsAnotherWindow`; no checkpoint is otherwise acceptable for v1.** This is exactly the mechanism intended for a step about to return a lesser product after banking earlier work ([another-window.ts:1](src/another-window.ts:1)). Stage 1 is already checkpointed before stage 2 ([structure.ts:3142](src/structure.ts:3142)), so handing back before any stage-2 call repeats no paid stage-1 work. **Change:** when eligible work exists, fewer than 120 seconds remain, and another window is available, throw `NeedsAnotherWindow`; skip only on the final/direct window. Once any stage-2 call starts, either admit the whole bounded wave or checkpoint completed batches—do not hand back partially without checkpoints.

**Verdict:** revise before building; the staged-call idea is sound, but the selector, response identity and process-wide gates are not yet safe enough to enable by default.

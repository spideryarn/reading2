F1 (P1 blocks) — `reportedRows` supports a narrower claim than the proposed sentences. Conversely, `reportedRows > 0` counts rows, not distinct pages “of” the returned pages: rows can be malformed, duplicate one URL, or name an unreturned URL ([types.ts:6891](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbqtk3q2-reception-messages/src/types.ts:6891)). Also, the actual display gate is `rows.length === 0`, not `counts.keptRows === 0` ([ReceptionAndClaimsPanel.tsx:665](/var/tmp/spideryarn-worktrees/fbqtk3q2-reception-messages/src/web/ReceptionAndClaimsPanel.tsx:665)).

Add red-first tests for first run, second-run skip, and re-extraction invalidation on both adapters. D0 is still the right first stage once this contract is corrected.

Keep the filesystem runner until every stage is converted. A partial D2 is safe for a week only under that filesystem runner—not after a Postgres cutover.

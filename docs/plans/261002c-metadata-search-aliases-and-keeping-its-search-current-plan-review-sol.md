Ranking by number of hit words before score is the right ranking *within* partial results, but it does not fix the wrong result set. A simpler relaxation preserves narrowing: after an AND miss, discard only query terms that match no section anywhere—`please` in `regenerate my glossary please`—then retry AND. If every term is known but no section answers them together, keep returning no result.

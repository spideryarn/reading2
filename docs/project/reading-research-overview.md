# Reading research: the evidence on reading and learning deeply

Up: [vision.md](vision.md)

The peer-reviewed evidence on how people read and learn deeply and efficiently, and what it says about
the way each Spideryarn mode is built. Greg asked for it on 2026-10-09:

> I'd like to think through and build up a library of peer-reviewed evidence about how to read and
> learn deeply and efficiently. I know a lot about learning and memory, but I don't know so much
> about reading per se. … I'm equally interested in research that suggests a better way to do
> things, as well as research that corroborates the way that we are doing things. And eventually we
> might turn some of this into marketing materials, and of course we'll use it to drive product
> development.

**This page is a map, not a summary.** The conclusions live in one place, the
[digest](../research/261010a-reading-and-learning-evidence/digest.md); the evidence and citations
live in the topic files. Open this page to find the right file. Before quoting any of it — in a
plan, a prompt or marketing copy — read the digest's § How to read the verdicts: almost none of this
research was done on expert adults reading papers, and most sources were read at abstract level.

## The topic files (2026-10-10)

Each answers one question, grades every finding for certainty and for how directly it applies to our
readers, and ends with what it means for each mode it touches.

**Learning from what you read**

- [01 Retrieval practice](../research/261010a-reading-and-learning-evidence/01-retrieval-practice.md)
  — recalling beats re-reading for keeping prose, if feedback follows; behind Recall and Quiz.
- [02 Pre-questions and metacomprehension](../research/261010a-reading-and-learning-evidence/02-prequestions-and-metacomprehension.md)
  — questions asked before reading help only what they ask; readers judge their own understanding
  poorly; behind Quiz, FAQ and Learn's feedback.
- [03 Scaffolding and desirable difficulty](../research/261010a-reading-and-learning-evidence/03-scaffolding-and-desirable-difficulty.md)
  — tutoring, the zone of proximal development, expertise reversal; behind Tutorial and the summary
  levels.
- [04 AI tutors and LLMs in learning](../research/261010a-reading-and-learning-evidence/04-ai-tutors-and-llms-in-learning.md)
  — the 2023–26 trials: help that does the work can leave you worse off afterwards.
- [08 Generating versus receiving](../research/261010a-reading-and-learning-evidence/08-generating-vs-receiving.md)
  — self-explanation, the generation effect, ICAP; the cross-cutting question for everything the
  model makes for the reader.
- [20 Purpose, motivation and personalisation](../research/261010a-reading-and-learning-evidence/20-purpose-motivation-personalisation.md)
  — a reading goal narrows what you learn; interest, curiosity, choice; behind the reader profile.

**AI text and the source text**

- [05 Summaries, organisers and coherence](../research/261010a-reading-and-learning-evidence/05-summaries-organisers-and-coherence.md)

- [06 Cognitive offloading](../research/261010a-reading-and-learning-evidence/06-cognitive-offloading.md)

- [07 Trusting AI text versus the source](../research/261010a-reading-and-learning-evidence/07-trusting-ai-text-vs-the-source.md)

- [18 Critical reading and sourcing](../research/261010a-reading-and-learning-evidence/18-critical-reading-and-sourcing.md)

## Adding to it

A new question gets its own file in the folder, in the topic files' shape (see the plan's § How each
researcher works), a line here, and its conclusions folded into the digest. A finding that changes a
verdict changes the digest, not just the topic file.

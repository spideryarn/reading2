## What the research found, in general

From research on 2026-10-09:

- **Metamuse is finished**: its last real episode was in October 2023.
- **The tools-for-thought podcast category has largely gone quiet since 2023.** Its listeners moved
  to a few general shows about how people use AI to think and work, and to newsletters.
- **The best fits are shows whose listeners read for a living**: AI-and-knowledge-work interviews,
  neuroscience-meets-AI, teaching in higher education, learning science, and researcher
  development. Pitch a cognition story (reading, memory, offloading) rather than a product.
- **Few shows publish a way to pitch**, so most need a direct message. Guest posts, newsletters
  and YouTube reviewers of research tools work much like podcasts and are listed alongside them.
- **Check a show is still running** before pitching. The research used the Apple Podcasts
  directory's newest-episode date, which was more reliable than web search.

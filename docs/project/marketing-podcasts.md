# Podcasts

Shows Greg could go on as a guest, ranked by how likely their listeners are to become readers who
would try Spideryarn and talk to him. Greg, 2026-10-09: *"I'm also good on podcasts … For instance,
the MetaMuse podcast with Adam Wiggins would have been perfect, and I know him a little bit, but I
think they've shut it down. So stuff like that, Tools for Thought, that kind of thing. Maybe tips for
scientists and academics."*

Apple Podcasts feed on that day**, which was more reliable than web-search summaries. Few shows
publish a way to pitch them, so most need a direct message.

## Metamuse, and why the category moved

**Metamuse is finished.** Its last real episode was 2023-10-05, then a hiatus notice in February
2024. Adam Wiggins now produces [localfirst.fm](https://www.localfirst.fm/), about local-first
sync engineering for developers, which has had only conference specials since mid-2025. Low fit.

The tools-for-thought podcast category has largely died since 2023: Tools for Thinking (Betaworks),
Building a Second Brain, the Obsidian shows and Feeling of Computing's own podcast are all quiet.
The audience moved to Cortex, Every and newsletters.

## Medium fit

- **Good audiences, harder to get on:** [The Cognitive Revolution](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-cognitive-revolution/id1669813431)
  (Nathan Labenz; AI early adopters, skewed to builders), [How I Write](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/how-i-write/id1700171470)
  (David Perell; serious readers, needs a warm introduction), [Clearer Thinking](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/clearer-thinking-with-spencer-greenberg/id1535406429)
  (Spencer Greenberg; heavy readers, by invitation).
- **Cognitive science, pitched as a cognition story rather than a product:**
  [Many Minds](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/many-minds/id1499167824) (Kensy Cooperrider),
  [The Learning Scientists](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-learning-scientists-podcast/id1279414575),
  [Two Psychologists Four Beers](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/two-psychologists-four-beers/id1387529624).
- **Teaching and PhD life:** [Tea for Teaching](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/tea-for-teaching/id1293488412),
  [Dead Ideas in Teaching and Learning](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/dead-ideas-in-teaching-and-learning/id1535499508)
  ("AI summaries replace reading" is a ready-made pitch),
  [On the Reg](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/on-the-reg/id1526042481) (Inger Mewburn; better
  as a guest post on her Thesis Whisperer blog, which has a big PhD readership),
  [The PhD Life Coach](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-phd-life-coach/id1650551306)
  (Vikki Wright, UK; mostly reruns lately).
- **Productivity and workflow:** [Focused](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/focused/id1138055739)
  and [Mac Power Users](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/mac-power-users/id458066753) (Relay),
  [Beyond the To-Do List](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/beyond-the-to-do-list-productivity-for-work-and-life/id554734474),
  [A Productive Conversation](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/a-productive-conversation/id913414044).
- **London:** [London Futurists](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/london-futurists/id1642832645)
  (Calum Chace, David Wood; augmentation versus automation fits, the listeners less so).

Lower: developer shows (Practical AI, Latent Space, Changelog, Hanselminutes) for the engineering
story, and edtech and workplace-learning shows (The Edtech Podcast, The Learning Hack, Learning
Uncut, ChatEDU, Mr Barton Maths), whose listeners buy for institutions rather than read for
themselves.

## Not podcasts, but they work like one

- **Ness Labs, Anne-Laure Le Cunff**: London, a neuroscientist at King's College London, a
  120k-subscriber newsletter and a paid community, written interviews with tools-for-thought
  founders, and public worry about AI eroding thinking. The best match on stance, and local. High.
- **Andy Stapleton on YouTube**: about 350k subscribers, reviews AI tools for researchers. Exactly
  the paper-reading users, but as a reviewer rather than a host. High for reach.
- **Guest posts**: Every, the Thesis Whisperer blog, Scholarly Kitchen (its podcast stopped in 2021;
  the blog runs pieces on AI and peer review).
- **London events with a podcast-like format**: the Future of Text symposium (Frode Hegland, yearly,
  2026 date unconfirmed), CUAK at Bush House (2026's theme was "tools"), Create With's "Does AI make
  us better thinkers?" series in Shoreditch. See also [marketing-talks.md](marketing-talks.md).

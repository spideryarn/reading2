# Bloggers and newsletter writers

Writers who might take a Zoom call with Greg and use Spideryarn themselves. Greg, 2026-10-09: *"at
this stage I'd probably rather do things that will help me get in contact with real individual
users. So maybe actually bloggers would be a good start because they might be up for a Zoom chat."*

## The approach

Researched 2026-10-09 by a web-search agent, which checked about forty writers against their own
sites that day.

- **Who:** people who read and write long, difficult material and are reachable: mid-sized rather
  than famous. Tools-for-thought writers, academics who blog (psychology, neuroscience, philosophy),
  metascience writers, and people who have written critically about AI summaries or for deep
  reading. UK-based is a plus, for coffee.
- **The opening:** a Spideryarn version of one of their own recent long pieces, by private link,
  asking what it gets wrong, and asking for twenty minutes
  ([marketing-author-gifts.md](marketing-author-gifts.md)).
- **What turned up:** several writers have recently published close to Spideryarn's own argument
  (AI that helps you read better rather than less), and one wrote about an AI peer-review
  experiment the day of the research. Those are the warmest openings.
- **Prior art worth reading:** Rachel Thomas, fast.ai,
  ["How To Use AI for the Ancient Art of Close Reading"](https://www.fast.ai/posts/2026-01-21-reading-LLMs/)
  (January 2026), the closest existing work to Spideryarn.

## Where these readers gather

The communities themselves (forums, Slacks, subreddits, salons) are listed in
`outreach-private/bloggers.md`. What holds for all of them: check each place's rules on
self-promotion before posting, and the safe framing everywhere is "I built this; tell me what's
wrong with it", not an advertisement.

See also [marketing-talks.md](marketing-talks.md) for in-person London events, several of which
share this crowd.

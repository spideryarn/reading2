**Shipped: the gap.** Since 2026-10-01 every row of Structure's list kept room on its right for the
band's (i) button, which sits only in the top row. That row already keeps clear of it on its own.
Under a finger the button is bigger, so the strip was about 55px of a 300px band: the gap in your
screenshot. It is gone, at every width the list is drawn. Titles wrap less, so at rung 4 the list
you were reading came out about 100px shorter, and that height goes to more of the tree.

**The wider look you asked for.** Screenshots and measurements at 725, 820, 1024, 1180, 1440 and
390, in Structure, Summary, Glossary and no mode (plan § The wider survey). The list's strip was
the only waste found at narrow widths. The margins either side of the prose at 1180 and 1440 are
the reading measure doing its job (about 60 characters, the cap you settled on 2026-10-23), so I
left them. One thing seen in the local copy, a figure's white sheet running past the window's
edge, could not be confirmed against production. I asked the Overseer to queue a check.

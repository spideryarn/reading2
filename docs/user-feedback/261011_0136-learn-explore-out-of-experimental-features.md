> The explore submode in learn should be a main mode, i.e. not an experimental feature anymore,

**Ending: Shipped.** It is on `dev` and not deployed.

"Main mode" was taken as *shown to everybody*: Explore is still one of Learn's four parts, not a
mode with its own button. If a button of its own was meant, say so and that is a separate piece of
work.

What we did:

- **Explore's chip is in every owner's Learn band**, switch on or off: Recall · Tutorial · Explore ·
  Quiz. Also in the band's (i) and as *Learn › Explore* in the command bar. Visitors see nothing
  new, since Learn is owners-only, and nothing is generated or paid for until the reader sends a
  message.
- **Tutorial now sends a reader's own line of thought to Explore**, not Chat. It pointed to Chat
  only because Explore was hidden. One eval run after the change: the turn where the reader asks
  for a web search answered briefly, named Explore, and went back to the article.
- `/help` no longer calls Explore experimental.

Nothing was deferred.

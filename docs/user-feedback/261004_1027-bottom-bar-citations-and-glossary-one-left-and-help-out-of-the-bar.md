**The order.** Glossary now comes before FAQ, and Citations before Referee. Each swapped with the
button on its left, inside its own group, so no separator line moved.

**Help.** It is gone from your bottom bar, on the reading view and on the Metadata page. You still
reach it two ways, both of which were already there: the command bar (⌘K, then *help*), and the
(i) at the top right of each mode, whose card ends in *More in Help →*. So "at the top of each
mode" needed nothing new.

**One thing to know, and to overrule if you like.** Somebody reading an article you shared has no
command bar, and in Plain no (i) either. Taking Help off their bar would leave them no way to it
from the page. So their bar keeps the Help link. If you would rather it went there too, it is one
condition to delete.

**Not built:** a help icon of its own beside each mode's (i). The (i) already carries the link,
and a third icon in that corner would move the clutter rather than remove it. Asking the command
bar a question is the further-off part you named as out of scope
([261003k](../plans/261003k-command-bar-takes-a-sentence-and-a-fast-model-picks-the-command.md)).

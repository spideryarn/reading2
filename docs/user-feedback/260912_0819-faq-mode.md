## What the reader said

> Add FAQ (frequently asked questions) mode as a new experimental features mode.

## What we did

**Shipped on `dev`, behind Experimental features, owner-only.** A new FAQ mode in the band: the
questions a careful first-time reader would put to the piece while reading it — the "wait, but…"
moments, not "what is this about" — each followed by the passages of the article where it responds,
quoted verbatim with a jump to the place.

**One call worth your eye: no written answers.** Fable and GPT Sol both argued, independently, that a
model-written answer under each question would be generated text standing in for prose the view
could show — the summary-in-question-form the vision rules out — and that nothing can check an answer
is supported by its passages. So the passages *are* the answers. The words are checked against the
article; which passage answers which question is the model's reading, and the band says so. Adding a
short written answer later is one field and one disclosure if you want it after using the mode.

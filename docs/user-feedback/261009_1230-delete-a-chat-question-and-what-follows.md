# Delete a chat question, and what follows it

Report #517, `spya-mx423m` (SPIDERYARN-READING2-FX), Greg, 2026-10-09 12:30 UTC, a suggestion filed
from Chat on the Attention paper (`arxiv-1706-03762-spya-wyt7j0`). Admin provenance proved by
`feedback-reporter.ts` (exit 0).

> There should be a way to delete a chat message. Like, I accidentally said, "Where should I
> start?" twice in this chat, and I want to remove the second one. I guess it would delete anything
> following.

**Shipped.** Each of your questions after the first has a bin beside its pencil. Press it once and
it says how many messages will go; press it again and that question, its answer and everything
after it are deleted. It is in every conversation that shows the pencil (Chat, the guide, Explore,
Tutorial, Learn, and the chat dialog), and it is withheld while an answer is arriving or Live is
talking. The first question has none: deleting it would empty the conversation, and the
conversation's own delete does that. The reasoning, the review and the tests are in
[261009m](../plans/261009m-delete-a-chat-question-and-what-follows.md). Overseer queue item
`qi-j4vfcasm`.

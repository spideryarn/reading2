# A banner on the shelf, on a phone: best on a larger screen

Report `spya-fcbnhq`, a suggestion, from Greg (admin), 2026-10-01, dispatched by the Overseer on
2026-10-02 with no Sentry mirror, on `https://www.spideryarn.com/changelog`:

> Spideryarn does not work that well on a mobile phone. It works, but because of the small screen
> the experience is suboptimal. So I wonder if we should show some kind of banner to people when
> they open it on a phone to say that it's probably best on a larger screen, and failing that, in
> Landscape mode.
>
> We'd show it on the logged-in homepage, and ideally only once/the first time they use it on a
> phone.

**Ending: Shipped**, on `dev`. Plan
[261002b](../plans/261002b-include-public-chip-on-the-shelf-empty-shelf-help-and-a-phone-banner-on-the-shelf.md)
§ Part C.

What changed: on a phone (a finger, and a screen whose short side is under 600px — so not an iPad,
and not a laptop window dragged narrow), the shelf opens with one line: *Spideryarn works best on a
larger screen — a tablet or a computer*, plus a suggestion to turn sideways when in portrait. The ×
puts it away for good on that device.

One deliberate difference from the ask: it shows **until dismissed** rather than only the first
time, which is the article banner's rule — a banner spent on a visit where the reader scrolled past
it has told them nothing. Asked back.

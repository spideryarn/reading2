What was chosen for ease and value. The research suggested a paid web search, like Reception's.
OpenAlex's semantic search on the piece's title and abstract returns the papers themselves, costs
$0.001 of the free daily allowance, and needs no AI. On *Attention Is All You Need* it found 23
attention and translation papers from 2017 or before, none of them in its bibliography. Two
safeguards: cited works are excluded against **our** Bibliography, because OpenAlex's own reference
list for that paper misses works it cites. And the search runs only on the owner's press, because it
sends the abstract to OpenAlex; the privacy page says so. Owner only, like Cited by.

Not built, and named in the plan: an AI-written line on why each paper is relevant, and a visitor's
view. At 390px the five Sources chips scroll sideways with a fade rather than wrapping, which is the
shared rule for every mode's chip row.

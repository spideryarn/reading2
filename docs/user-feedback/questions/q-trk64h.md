When you gift an article that was imported on the normal model (Sonnet), should the High-powered AI box redo its main modes on Opus, or only use Opus for whatever is still to be made?

A. Opus from now on only. This is what is built. Nothing already made is redone, so on an article whose modes exist the box changes almost nothing you can see. To have a gift all on Opus, tick High-powered AI when you import it (the add page, or For the author…, which ticks it for you). Costs nothing extra.
B. Redo the main modes on Opus when the box is ticked (recommended). The gift is read by the one person certain to notice a mistake, and about $1.50 a gift is small. Structure and the section summaries stay as imported. About a day's work: Glossary and Quotes today add a second batch when re-run instead of replacing the first, so they need a "replace" run first.
C. B, and rebuild Structure on Opus too. The whole article on Opus, about $2 a gift. While it rebuilds, the paragraph labels disappear for about ten minutes, and it is the part with the most risk; more work than B.

Details

On 2026-10-21 you asked, from /admin/vouchers (report #668): "Provide an option for me to set author-gift/starter articles to be imported with high-powered AI. Oh, and by default, any articles that we share as part of this should automatically get their main modes generated as well."

What shipped: every new gift (the Give form, For the author… on the add page, and an agent's draft through MCP), and every voucher with a starter article, now queues the main modes for its article: Summary (plain words and the thread), Glossary, Quotes, Ideas, Skim, Sources' Bibliography, and the cross-reference links. A mode already made is skipped at no cost. It ignores your own "Generate the main modes" import setting, because that one is about your reading. The Give form has a High-powered AI tick box, on by default, and each draft gift card says whether its article is on High-powered AI, with a button to switch it on.

The catch is in option A. Switching High-powered AI on changes the model for what is made afterwards; it never redoes what exists, and the app counts a Sonnet-made mode as current. Your articles get their modes when you import them, so by the time you gift one, most of it is already Sonnet's. The tick box then mostly does nothing.

Costs, from the last three weeks of real imports (median per article): the import itself is about $0.36 on Sonnet and $0.55 on Opus; the main modes about $1.03 on Sonnet and $1.77 on Opus. So a gift imported on Sonnet is about $1.40 today; B makes it about $2.90; an article imported on Opus from the start is about $2.30.

What would decide it: how often you gift an article you imported before you decided to gift it. If it's usually "import it for the author" from the start, A plus ticking at import is enough. If you pick from your existing shelf, B is what the box should mean.

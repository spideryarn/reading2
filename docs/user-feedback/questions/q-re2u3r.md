Background. You asked whether a signed-in reader should be able to use AI, search for example, on a public article that is not theirs, and said to go ahead only if it did not add complexity. It does, so nothing is built. We would pay for those AI calls, not the article's owner, and nothing limits them today. Every version that runs AI in place on someone else's article also changes a listed security defence, which an unattended agent does not do.

Question 1. Which of these, if any?

A. Leave it. A signed-in visitor reads what the owner made, and cannot search.

B. A button for a signed-in visitor: Add a private copy to your shelf. One press uses one of their articles and starts the import. About an afternoon. Changes no security defence.

C. A quick search in place for a signed-in reader, nothing saved, with a daily limit. A few sessions. Changes security defences.

D. Go straight to the full design: private searches and chats for every reader.

E. The free words search for every visitor. Not AI. About an afternoon. Changes no security defence.

What would decide it: if the point is that a signed-in reader should not hit a dead end, B does that. If the point is that they should be able to ask this article something without leaving it, only C or D does.

Recommended: B and E now, then C.

Question 2. If C: is a signed-in reader's quick search free with a daily limit, or does it count against their allowance?

A. Free, with a daily limit per person and a ceiling for everybody, both counted in AI calls. Simple. At $0.0006 a call, a ceiling of, say, 5,000 calls a day for everybody is $3 a day at the very worst. The allowance stays a count of articles added.

B. It counts against their allowance: for example, the first search on a public article uses a fraction of an article. Ties the spending to the plan they pay for. Costs: a new kind of entry in the billing records, and somebody has to explain to a reader why searching another person's article used up some of theirs.

What would decide it: whether you see a search on a public article as a taste that brings people in (A), or as part of what a plan buys (B).

Recommended: A.

Not asked now: who pays for D. Its searches and chats cost about a hundred times more per call and nothing limits them, so that waits on a general spending limit, which does not exist yet.

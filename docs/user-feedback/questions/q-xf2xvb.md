Background. Your report shipped as Dig deeper on the citation card. This is its larger half, and none of it is built. You asked for two things: fewer major modes, since Citations and Debate are both about other people's work; and a way to see where a cited work sits in the wider debate.

Today Citations lists the works the article cites. Debate has two parts: Reception, what others wrote about the piece, and Claims, what has been written about each claim it makes. Nothing links the two modes.

Question 1. How far to go?

A. Keep two modes and connect them with buttons. Dig deeper was the first of these. Costs almost nothing. Gives up: the number of modes stays the same.

B. One mode with three parts: Reception, Claims, Cited works. The citations list moves, as it is, under Debate. One fewer button on the mode bar, and old links keep working. Costs: a few days. Gives up nothing a reader has today, but by itself it only puts the three lists side by side.

C1. An addition to A or to B. In Claims, each claim lists the works the article cites in that claim's paragraph, each with its verdict where it has one. It needs no AI call. It shows only that they share a paragraph, so the heading would say "cited in this paragraph", not "supports this claim".

C2. A further addition. A model files each cited work under one of Debate's themes, so a citation can open the theme it belongs to. Costs: a new AI step, and a new way to be wrong, because for most cited works we have only the bibliography entry.

Recommended: B, then C1; not C2 yet.

Question 2. If B: Debate is behind the Experimental switch and Citations is not. Does the merged mode come out from behind the switch, or does only its Cited works part show with the switch off?

Question 3. The name of the merged mode: Sources (recommended), Discussion, References, or keep Debate. Debate is the wrong word for a bibliography, and References is the wrong word for replies.

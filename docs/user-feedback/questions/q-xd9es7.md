Should Marginalia, the column of notes to the right of the article, get a filter so you can say which kinds of note it shows?

A. Not yet (recommended). Leave the column as it is. Each paragraph gets at most one line of each kind, and most kinds start shut, so it is rarely crowded. Look again if it starts to feel busy.

B. A row of switches at the top of the column, one per kind of note: Ideas, FAQ, Timeline, Debate, Citations, Comments. Remembered on this device. About a day of work. Costs one more row of controls in a column that is narrow already.

Recommended: A.

Details

Where this came from. On 3 Oct you asked for fewer modes, and suggested putting Glossary, Ideas and Quotes inside Marginalia, "so that you could say, I only want..." those notes. Two smaller ideas were proposed instead. One was a single bar button for the list modes; that was answered on 7 Oct by the More button, which now holds Quotes, Glossary, FAQ, Ideas and Timeline. This is the other one, and it was never asked in a way you could answer here.

Why not put the list modes inside Marginalia. Marginalia is a column beside the article, not a band below it, and it is hidden on a phone. Moving Glossary, Ideas and Quotes into it would take them away on a phone.

What Marginalia shows today. A relation word (so, but, vs) where the argument turns; a pinned line saying which part you are in; each part's question; a stamp where an idea first appears; and, shut until pressed, lines from FAQ, Timeline, Debate, Citations and comments, each from a mode that has already run. Glossary terms are marked in the article itself, not in the column, so "only glossary notes" would not be one of the switches.

Your three other follow-ups from that report are settled: Tweets is Summary's Thread; the Simple summary level has gone; and More gathers the list modes.

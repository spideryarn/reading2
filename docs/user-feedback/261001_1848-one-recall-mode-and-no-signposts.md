> I think let's get rid of a signposts mode because in the previous feedback I suggested that we
> want to just tweak the main default mode to provide more block links. I was also a bit
> disappointed by the Socratic mode. Perhaps do some web research on Socratic question best
> practices in education, like what makes for a good Socratic question.
>
> I think what I was hoping for was a bit more of like a nudge. Hey, do you remember when they talked
> about this? Or why did they say that? Or do you remember anything further about it?
>
> Or yeah, so in other words, giving me a clue that will make it likely that I will actually respond
> successfully without making it kind of a gimme or trivial or patronizing. And as before, I think we
> want things to be briefer in its response. I think basically I want to get to the point where
> actually there's really only one recall mode, which is fairly brief, simple language makes use of
> block links and tries to keep nudging me with hints and questions so that I'm constantly
> remembering a bit more and a bit more because the act of recollection is what helps learning.
>
> And so the job, your job, the job is to prompt the reader, you know, the user in such a way that
> they're continually recalling the way that they're not going to be able to recall. And so I think
> that's a lot of people recalling a little bit more each time.
>
> And you know what, if it's clear that they are struggling, then don't make them suffer or feel bad
> or fail. In that case, maybe you do just provide more. So I guess you're being a bit adaptive. And
> that's why we only need one mode.
